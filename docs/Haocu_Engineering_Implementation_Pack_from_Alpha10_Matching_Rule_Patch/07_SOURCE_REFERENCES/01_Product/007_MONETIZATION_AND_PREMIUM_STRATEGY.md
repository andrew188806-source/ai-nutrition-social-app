# Monetization and Premium Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how Haocu can monetize without damaging trust, product clarity, or MVP focus.

## Monetization Philosophy

Haocu should monetize depth and convenience, not basic food understanding.

The free product must prove the core loop. Premium should unlock:

- more daily opportunities;
- richer identity;
- longer memory;
- deeper personalization;
- advanced social and health-goal features.

## Consumer Premium Strategy

### Free Tier

Free users should receive:

- AI meal analysis flow;
- meal record and today summary;
- anonymous mascot social card;
- limited daily meal-buddy card opportunities;
- limited candidate list;
- basic restaurant recommendations;
- limited saved history window.

Free should feel useful enough to share.

### Premium Tier

Premium can unlock:

- real-person card where verification boundary is satisfied;
- more meal-buddy card opportunities per day;
- more candidate recommendations;
- multi-select invite where defined;
- longer saved history or Top saved items;
- health-goal mode;
- advanced restaurant/taste personalization;
- group table upgrades where appropriate.

### Premium Rules from Current Product Decisions

- Free: anonymous card, daily 2 card opportunities, smaller candidate lists, limited list sizes, shorter saved window.
- Premium: real-person card option, daily 5 card opportunities, larger candidate list, multi-select where defined, Top saved items, health-goal mode.
- If a card limit is reached, replacement behavior should be explicit and predictable.

## Restaurant Monetization Strategy

Restaurant monetization should be phased carefully.

### MVP / Early Phase

- Restaurant card visibility.
- Structured menu/dish participation.
- Potential pilot partnerships.
- No aggressive paid placement that damages recommendation trust.

### MVP+ / Beta

- Restaurant profile/admin tools.
- Menu nutrition review support.
- Campaign surfaces with clear labeling.
- Analytics for restaurant demand signals.

### Future

- ESG/surplus food operations.
- B2B restaurant data tools.
- Supply and demand forecasting.
- Community/household fulfillment surfaces.

## Crowdfunding Bridge

Crowdfunding can validate demand before large-scale fundraising.

Potential consumer reward types:

- early premium membership;
- mascot blind box / acrylic stand / keychain;
- founder badge or early supporter title;
- invitation quota or friend rewards;
- food personality-test result expansion.

Potential restaurant reward types:

- early restaurant profile package;
- featured pilot partner page;
- limited menu data setup support;
- early analytics preview.

## Trust Guardrails

Haocu must not create a conflict between recommendation quality and paid placement.

Required rules:

1. Paid or sponsored content must be labeled.
2. Nutrition claims require professional review.
3. Restaurant ranking should not be secretly pay-to-win.
4. User health data must not be sold as individual-level targeting data.
5. Premium upsell should not clutter the core flow.
6. Free users should retain the core analyze/save/recommend loop.

## Pricing Questions for Future Validation

Before final pricing, validate:

- Which premium feature users value most.
- Whether users pay for social identity or nutrition personalization.
- Whether restaurant partners understand the value of structured dish data.
- Whether crowdfunding backers prefer app access or mascot/IP rewards.
- Whether monthly subscription or annual founder plan performs better.

## Monetization Success Metrics

| Metric | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Free activation rate | Whether the core loop works before payment. |
| Paywall impression to upgrade intent | Whether premium benefits are understandable. |
| Premium conversion | Whether users value advanced features. |
| Retention by tier | Whether premium increases habit. |
| Restaurant partner leads | Whether restaurants see business value. |
| Crowdfunding conversion | Whether public story converts into early cash. |
