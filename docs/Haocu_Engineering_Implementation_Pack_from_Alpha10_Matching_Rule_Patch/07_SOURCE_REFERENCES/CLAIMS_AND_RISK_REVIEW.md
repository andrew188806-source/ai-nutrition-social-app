# Claims and Risk Review

## Purpose

This file identifies claim categories that require evidence, disclaimers, or professional review before external use.

## Claims Status Table

| Claim Type | Acceptable Draft Framing | Requires Evidence / Review Before External Use |
|---|---|---|
| Product existence | “The repository defines the planned MVP and demo flow.” | “The app is live,” unless live production evidence exists. |
| AI nutrition | “AI estimates meal content and nutrition with user correction.” | Accuracy percentages, medical-grade claims, or disease-related guidance. |
| Personalization | “Designed to learn taste and meal preferences over time.” | Claims that it reliably predicts health outcomes or guarantees preference matching. |
| Restaurant data | “Restaurant partner workflow is planned and pilot-ready.” | Signed restaurant partnerships, verified menu data, or commercial contracts. |
| Social matching | “Meal Buddy is designed to help users find dining companions.” | Safety guarantees, dating claims, or abuse-prevention certainty. |
| Market opportunity | “The target market is Taiwan-first external food consumers and restaurant partners.” | Market-size numbers without source, methodology, and date. |
| Traction | “Evidence trackers are provided for future demo/pilot proof.” | User counts, revenue, LOIs, pilots, retention, conversion, or CAC without data. |
| Fundraising | “Materials support investor conversations.” | Securities offering, guaranteed return, valuation certainty, or investment performance. |
| Legal/IP | “IP and patent review paths are prepared.” | “Patented,” “protected,” “non-copyable,” or legally cleared statements. |
| Compliance | “Privacy, consent, and nutrition-claim review areas are identified.” | “Compliant” without legal/privacy review. |

## Red-Flag Claims

The following should be removed or rewritten unless supported by professional review and evidence:

- “Guaranteed nutrition accuracy.”
- “Medical-grade AI.”
- “Clinically proven.”
- “Patented technology” unless granted or filed and counsel approves wording.
- “Restaurants have signed” unless the signed document exists.
- “Users love it” unless supported by user research notes.
- “Live traction” if only demo/mock data exists.
- “No legal risk.”
- “Safe social dining guaranteed.”
- “Investors will get X return.”

## Required Disclaimer Themes

Use appropriate disclaimers in external materials:

- AI nutrition outputs are estimates and may be wrong.
- Users can correct meal analysis results.
- Haocu does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or prescription diets.
- Social dining features require safety, consent, reporting, and moderation controls.
- Investor materials may contain forward-looking statements and assumptions.
- Legal/IP/privacy/fundraising statements require professional review.

## Evidence Required Before Stronger Claims

| Strong Claim | Evidence Needed |
|---|---|
| Live users | Analytics export, user database count, date range, definition of active user. |
| Restaurant pilots | Signed LOI, meeting notes, pilot plan, restaurant approval, scope. |
| AI accuracy | Test set, methodology, benchmark, error analysis, reviewer signoff. |
| Revenue | Payment records, accounting summary, date range. |
| Retention | Cohort analysis, event definitions, date range. |
| Legal/IP protection | Filing receipt, legal memo, trademark search, patent counsel guidance. |
| Privacy compliance | Privacy review, data inventory, consent flow, retention/deletion policy. |

## Investor Communication Rule

When unsure, say: “This is planned/demo-stage unless otherwise marked. We are tracking evidence separately and will update the data room as evidence becomes available.”

## Social Matching Risk Patch

Meal Buddy ranking should avoid repeatedly surfacing users who already accepted, chatted, or became active Meal Buddy contacts. This reduces duplicate recommendations, social fatigue, and perceived stalking/harassment risk. Re-surfacing users who ignored or declined invitations should be down-ranked and optionally cooled down; penalty logic should remain internal and not be framed as a negative judgment about either user.
