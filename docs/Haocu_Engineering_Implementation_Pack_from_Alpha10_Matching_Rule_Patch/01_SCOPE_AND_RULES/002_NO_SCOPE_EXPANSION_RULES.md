# 002 No Scope Expansion Rules

## Engineering Rule

Build the frozen MVP baseline. Do not expand from fundraising language, mascot ideas, future restaurant/ESG concepts, or investor imagination unless the founder explicitly creates a new build decision.

## How to Classify New Ideas

Any new request must be labeled before it enters engineering backlog:

- `MVP Required`
- `MVP Nice-to-Have`
- `MVP+`
- `Future Phase`
- `Parking Lot`

Unlabeled ideas must not become engineering tickets.

## Examples of Do Not Build Now

- full restaurant POS replacement
- full restaurant supply-chain / leftover food logistics
- household fresh food app
- live payment integration
- ad marketplace
- production identity verification vendor
- advanced ML taste graph beyond MVP placeholder

## When in Doubt

Ask: “Is this required to prove the MVP loop?” If not, keep it out of sprint scope.
