# Food Intelligence Theory

Status: Draft v1.0
Priority: P0 Foundation

## Definition

Food Intelligence 是持續理解食物、使用者、餐廳與飲食行為，並協助做出更好食物決策的能力。

## Principles

1. Food Decisions Are Contextual
2. Food Decisions Are Continuous
3. Food Decisions Are Personal
4. Food Decisions Learn From Feedback
5. Food Decisions Balance Health And Preference
6. Food Decisions Include Social Context
7. Food Decisions Improve Over Time
