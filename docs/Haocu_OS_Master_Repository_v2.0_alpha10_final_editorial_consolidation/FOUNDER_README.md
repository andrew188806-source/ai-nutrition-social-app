# Founder README

## Purpose

This file tells the founder how to use Alpha 10 as the final working command center for Haocu OS before handing the repository to engineers, investors, lawyers, advisors, or restaurant partners.

## Founder Operating Rule

Use Alpha 10 as the stable baseline. Do not continue adding product modules inside this repository just because a new idea appears. Capture new ideas separately, decide whether they belong to MVP, post-MVP, investor evidence, legal review, or future roadmap, and only reopen the repository with an explicit version change.

## Founder Responsibilities Before Sharing

- Decide the audience and share only the appropriate layer.
- Do not send raw internal notes to investors or restaurant partners unless intentionally reviewed.
- Keep evidence labels honest: Planned, Demo, Pilot, Live, Verified.
- Replace placeholders before external use.
- Confirm whether the recipient needs the full repository, the clean data room, or a role-specific subset.
- Maintain a log of who received which files and when.

## What Not To Do

- Do not tell investors that pilots, live traction, LOIs, AI accuracy, legal clearance, or restaurant partnerships exist unless evidence is recorded.
- Do not treat PRD language as legal contract language.
- Do not treat mock data as user traction.
- Do not ask engineers to build every future idea before the MVP baseline is stable.
- Do not let restaurant-partner conversations expand MVP scope without founder approval.
