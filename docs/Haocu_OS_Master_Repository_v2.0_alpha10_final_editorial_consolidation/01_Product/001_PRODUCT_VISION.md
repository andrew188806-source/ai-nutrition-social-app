# Product Vision

## One-Sentence Vision

Haocu helps Taiwan external diners decide what to eat, understand how each meal fits their body and routine, and optionally turn eating into a lighter social experience.

## Product Thesis

Most food products solve only one part of the eating decision:

- calorie trackers ask users to manually log too much;
- map apps rank restaurants by public popularity rather than personal taste;
- social dining apps often lack food intent, safety boundaries, and nutrition context;
- restaurant tools rarely translate menu data into personalized consumer guidance.

Haocu combines these layers into one daily loop:

1. User records or analyzes a meal.
2. Haocu explains nutrition in simple language.
3. Haocu learns taste and eating behavior.
4. Haocu recommends the next meal or restaurant based on personal fit.
5. The user may invite or join meal buddies around the same intent.
6. Restaurants can later contribute structured dish and nutrition data.

The strategic moat is not generic AI recognition. The moat is the accumulated relationship between personal taste, Taiwanese restaurant data, meal context, nutrition state, and social dining intent.

## Mission

Make everyday eating decisions easier, more personal, and less lonely without turning food into a medicalized or shame-based experience.

## What Haocu Is

Haocu is:

- a meal analysis assistant;
- a personal food memory and taste graph;
- a next-meal and restaurant recommendation engine;
- a privacy-aware meal-buddy social layer;
- a restaurant data surface for future nutrition transparency and business operations.

## What Haocu Is Not

Haocu is not:

- a medical nutrition diagnosis system;
- a hospital diet management product;
- a precision bodybuilding macro tracker;
- a POS replacement for restaurants during MVP;
- a generic dating app;
- a pure calorie counter;
- a public review platform competing only on star ratings.

## Core Positioning

### Consumer Positioning

For Taiwan external diners who often need fast meal decisions, Haocu provides AI-assisted food analysis and personalized recommendations that consider taste, nutrition, meal timing, and optional social intent.

### Restaurant Positioning

For restaurants, Haocu starts as a consumer discovery and meal-intent surface. Over time, it becomes a structured dish, nutrition, and demand signal platform that can support restaurant visibility, menu optimization, and ESG-related operations.

### Investor Positioning

Haocu is a consumer AI application with restaurant-side data expansion. Its long-term defensibility depends on proprietary user taste data, localized food data, and the interaction between meal records, recommendation, and social conversion.

## Long-Term Product Arc

### Phase 1 — MVP Consumer App

- AI meal analysis.
- Meal records and food diary.
- Next-meal recommendation.
- Restaurant recommendation.
- Meal-buddy card and basic matching.
- Premium/free distinction.
- Demo-ready restaurant surfaces.

### Phase 2 — Restaurant Data and ESG Operations

- Restaurant menu and dish database.
- Nutrition transparency review flow.
- Restaurant campaign surfaces.
- Surplus/near-expiry food operations design.
- Restaurant demand and supply signals.

### Phase 3 — Household Nutrition App

- Family meal planning.
- Community pickup or fresh food pack concepts.
- Household nutrition gap recommendation.
- Integration of restaurant and retail supply signals.

## Product Differentiation

| Competing Pattern | Their Weakness | Haocu Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Calorie tracker | Manual, boring, hard to sustain | Photo-first, conversational, restaurant-aware |
| Map/review app | Public popularity may not match user taste | Personal taste and similar-user signals |
| Food delivery app | Optimized for purchase, not health or routine | Meal fit, nutrition balance, and social intent |
| Dating/social app | Weak meal context and safety intent | Food-first, opt-in, bounded social interaction |
| Restaurant SaaS | Restaurant-first setup burden | Consumer-first demand signal before deeper restaurant tooling |

## MVP Success Definition

The MVP succeeds if a first-time demo user can complete this path without explanation:

1. Open the app.
2. Start AI analysis by taking or uploading a meal photo.
3. See candidate dishes and nutrition estimate.
4. Correct or confirm the result.
5. Save the meal into today’s intake.
6. See a simple nutrition summary.
7. Receive a restaurant or next-meal recommendation.
8. Create or view a meal-buddy card.
9. Understand free vs premium differences.

## Product Experience Tone

Haocu should feel:

- clean and uncluttered;
- warm and lightly cute;
- trustworthy but not medical;
- Taiwanese and external-dining aware;
- helpful rather than judgmental;
- easy to demo to both young users and older business stakeholders.

## Strategic Product Moat

The future moat is built from:

1. Localized Taiwanese dish and restaurant data.
2. Personal food rating and correction history.
3. Similar taste-user recommendation graph.
4. Meal-buddy intent and conversion data.
5. Restaurant menu and supply-side signals.
6. Brand/IP layer through mascots and shareable identity.

## Product North Star

A user should be able to ask Haocu, implicitly or explicitly:

> “Given what I ate, what I like, where I am, and whether I want company, what should I eat next?”

Everything in the MVP should support this question.
