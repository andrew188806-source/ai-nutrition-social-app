# Restaurant Product Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how restaurants fit into Haocu without distracting from the consumer MVP.

## Restaurant Role in Haocu

Restaurants are not merely listings. They are sources of:

- structured dishes;
- nutrition assumptions;
- taste and rating signals;
- meal-buddy locations;
- future demand forecasting;
- future ESG/supply-chain operations.

## MVP Restaurant Strategy

The MVP should use restaurant surfaces to improve consumer value.

### Required MVP Surfaces

- Restaurant list.
- Restaurant card.
- Plausible menu/dish examples.
- Restaurant recommendation explanation.
- Create meal-buddy card from restaurant card.

### MVP Design Rules

- Restaurant cards should not be visually overloaded.
- Date/time selector for card creation must appear near the restaurant context.
- Restaurant dishes must match restaurant type.
- “Use this meal to find meal buddy” should not duplicate existing actions.
- Group table entry should exist only if visually calm and logically clear.

## Restaurant Data Sources

Possible data sources over time:

1. Founder-curated seed data.
2. Publicly visible restaurant menu information where legally and operationally appropriate.
3. User AI uploads associated with restaurant context.
4. Restaurant-submitted menu data.
5. Admin-reviewed nutrition estimates.
6. Future POS or ordering integrations.

## Restaurant Partner Value Proposition

For restaurants, Haocu can eventually offer:

- discovery by personal fit rather than broad ranking;
- dish-level visibility;
- nutrition transparency support;
- meal-buddy/social demand generation;
- early customer insight;
- campaign and membership surfaces;
- future surplus/near-expiry demand channel.

## Restaurant Trust Boundary

Haocu must distinguish:

- estimated nutrition;
- restaurant-provided information;
- professionally reviewed information;
- sponsored promotion;
- organic recommendation.

These states must not be visually or legally confused.

## Restaurant MVP+ Backlog Themes

- Restaurant admin dashboard.
- Menu editing.
- Dish nutrition review queue.
- Restaurant verification.
- Campaign creation.
- Restaurant analytics.
- Partner onboarding workflow.

## Future Strategy

Restaurant data creates the foundation for Phase 2 and Phase 3:

```text
Restaurant dish data
  -> Personalized restaurant recommendation
  -> Demand signal
  -> Restaurant partner tools
  -> Surplus/near-expiry operations
  -> Household nutrition fulfillment
```

The consumer app should therefore collect data in a way that does not block these future paths.
