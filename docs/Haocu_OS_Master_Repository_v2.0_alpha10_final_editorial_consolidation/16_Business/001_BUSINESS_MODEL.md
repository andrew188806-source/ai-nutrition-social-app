# Business Model

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 6.
> Scope: Business, Legal/IP, Finance, Pitch, Investor Materials, External Artifacts, and final Repository Packaging.
> Audience: founder, CTO, engineers, designers, legal counsel, accountants, advisors, crowdfunding partners, and investors.

## Cross-Repository Boundaries

- **Taiwan-first MVP**: consumer mobile app, AI meal analysis, meal records, restaurant discovery, Meal Buddy cards, chat/invitations, group tables, premium limits, restaurant/admin tools, and clean demo readiness are the baseline.
- **Database-first AI**: verified restaurant/menu/nutrition data is preferred before free-form AI inference; AI output must expose uncertainty and correction paths.
- **Nutrition boundary**: Haocu provides food information and meal guidance, not medical diagnosis, prescription dieting, disease treatment, or guaranteed health outcomes.
- **Social safety boundary**: Meal Buddy and group dining features must protect identity, consent, harassment prevention, meeting safety, moderation, and abuse reporting.
- **Professional review boundary**: legal, patent, trademark, securities, accounting, tax, nutrition claims, and valuation matters require qualified external review before external publication or execution.
- **Clean UI principle**: Haocu should remain tidy, readable, spacious, demo-friendly, and free from duplicate controls.


## Objective

Define how Haocu can become a company while preserving the MVP loop: meal photo analysis, user correction, private taste/nutrition memory, recommendation, optional Meal Buddy, and restaurant/menu data.

## Operating Principles

- Monetization must reinforce trust, not interrupt it.
- Free users must experience the core loop; Premium should add depth, limits, memory, identity options, and convenience.
- Restaurants are data and discovery partners, not just ad buyers.
- Phase 2 supply-chain and Phase 3 household nutrition remain roadmap, not MVP promises.

## Requirements

| Area | Decision / Requirement | Review / Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer Premium | Higher Meal Buddy limits, longer diary memory, Top10, real-person card after verification, more recommendation depth. | Must match PRD/Data/Backend/UI free/Premium limits. |
| Restaurant tools | Restaurant profile, menu structure, QR/nutrition page, reviewed/verified data workflow. | No guaranteed traffic or medical-grade nutrition certification. |

| Defensible assets | Corrected meal records, taste memory, restaurant/menu graph, food-social intent, mascot identity. | Protect through data governance, IP assignment, trademark/patent review, and trade secrets. |

## Core Business Loop

1. User records or analyzes a meal.
2. Haocu estimates and asks for correction instead of pretending AI is perfect.
3. Corrected data improves food diary, private taste memory, and nutrition context.
4. Recommendations become more personal than generic public ratings.
5. Meal Buddy and group table flows turn food intent into optional social action.
6. Restaurants improve local data supply and discovery quality.

## Non-Negotiable Business Boundaries

- Do not claim medical outcomes.
- Do not hide sponsorship inside “AI recommendation.”
- Do not sell personal health/social data in ways users would find surprising.
- Do not let mascot/crowdfunding goods distract from product validation.
