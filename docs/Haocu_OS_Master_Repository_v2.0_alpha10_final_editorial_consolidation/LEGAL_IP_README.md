## External Sharing Rule

Before any public website, pitch deck, crowdfunding page, investor memo, restaurant LOI, partner agreement, privacy policy, terms of service, patent filing, or trademark filing is used externally, counsel should identify which statements require edits, disclaimers, evidence, or removal.

## High-Risk Language To Review

- “Patented,” “patent-protected,” or “cannot be copied.”
- “Clinically accurate,” “medical-grade,” “guaranteed nutrition accuracy,” or disease/therapy claims.
- “Signed partner,” “pilot confirmed,” “traction,” “revenue,” or “verified” without evidence.
- “Safe dating,” “guaranteed safe meal partner,” or unsupported safety guarantees.
- Valuation, return, upside, or investment-performance claims.

## Recommended Legal Output

Ask reviewers to return:

- Redline comments by document path.
- Approved / revise / remove status for claims.
- IP filing recommendation and disclosure timing.
- Privacy/terms consent checklist.
- Fundraising and restaurant-pilot language guardrails.
- List of documents safe for external use after edits.
