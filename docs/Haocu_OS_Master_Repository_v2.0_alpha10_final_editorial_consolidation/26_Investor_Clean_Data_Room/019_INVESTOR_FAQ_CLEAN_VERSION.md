## Investor FAQ

### What is Haocu?

Haocu is a Taiwan-first AI food recommendation platform for daily outside eaters. It starts with meal analysis and private food memory, then recommends what to eat next and optionally helps users find Meal Buddies or group tables.

### Is this a nutrition app, restaurant app, or social app?

It is food recommendation first. Nutrition awareness, restaurant discovery, and optional social dining are supporting layers. The product should still be useful even if a user never uses social features.

### What is the core insight?

Generic restaurant ratings do not know the user. Haocu can become more personal because it learns from the user’s own corrected meals, taste patterns, recent intake, and restaurant data.

### Why start in Taiwan?

Taiwan has dense outside-eating behavior, strong restaurant variety, and a natural fit for Traditional Chinese mobile UX and local restaurant discovery. The first launch should be Taiwan-first before broader expansion.

### What is in the MVP?

AI meal analysis, correction, meal diary, next-meal recommendation, Meal Buddy card, basic group table/chat/invite flow, restaurant/dish cards, Premium limits, and admin/review foundations.

### What is not in the MVP?

Medical diet treatment, full supply-chain/ESG operations, household nutrition planning, complex restaurant analytics, and broad international expansion are later phases.

### How does Haocu make money?

Potential revenue includes consumer Premium, crowdfunding support, restaurant partner packages, and later B2B/household expansions. The first validation should test user willingness to pay, restaurant interest, and crowdfunding conversion.

### Is the AI defensible?

The AI alone is not the full moat. Defensibility may come from corrected meal data loops, taste memory, restaurant data relationships, product execution, brand, trust/safety, and possible counsel-reviewed IP strategy.

### What are the main risks?

AI accuracy, privacy, health/nutrition claims, social safety, restaurant adoption, monetization, crowdfunding execution, and founder bandwidth. These are addressed through MVP staging, correction flows, professional review, pilot testing, and clean scope control.

### Are there legal or compliance issues?

Yes, and they are being treated as review workstreams. Public claims, privacy/terms, nutrition wording, fundraising language, patent/trademark strategy, and restaurant partner terms require professional review.

### What funding is needed?

The clean framing is milestone-based: fund the build and review work required to reach MVP, closed pilot, restaurant partner evidence, crowdfunding readiness, and stronger next-round metrics. Exact amount and terms require finance/legal review.

### Why can this become more than a feature?

Because the product can connect repeated consumer behavior, private meal memory, personalized recommendations, restaurant data, optional social dining, and future B2B workflows. The MVP tests the first loop; later phases depend on evidence.

### What evidence should investors expect next?

Stable demo, MVP core loop, pilot user data, recommendation usefulness, correction behavior, restaurant partner feedback, Premium/crowdfunding interest, reviewed external claims, and updated data room metrics.

### What do you need from angels/advisors?

Capital, product discipline, technical review, restaurant access, legal/IP/privacy guidance, finance/crowdfunding review, growth mentorship, and help turning the repository into executed milestones.
