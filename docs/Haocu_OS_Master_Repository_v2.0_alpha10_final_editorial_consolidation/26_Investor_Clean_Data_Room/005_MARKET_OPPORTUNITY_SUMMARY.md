## Market Thesis

Haocu targets a common and repeated behavior: daily outside eating in Taiwan. The opportunity is not only “food tracking” or “restaurant discovery.” It sits between food decision-making, nutrition awareness, personal taste memory, restaurant data, and optional meal-based social interaction.

## Why This Market Can Be Attractive

- Eating decisions happen frequently, giving the product many opportunities to be useful.
- Restaurant discovery is still noisy because public ratings do not represent individual fit.
- Many users want healthier or more intentional food choices without becoming medical or fitness power users.
- AI photo analysis lowers the friction of meal recording compared with manual entry.
- Social products can feel stressful; meal-based intent provides a more natural reason to interact.
- Restaurants increasingly need better digital discovery and structured dish-level communication.

## Initial Beachhead

The initial market should be narrow enough to execute:

| Segment | Why It Fits MVP |
|---|---|
| Taiwan urban outside eaters | Frequent dining decisions and dense restaurant supply. |
| Young professionals / students | High app comfort, repeated meal decisions, social openness. |
| Nutrition-curious but not medical users | Need guidance without strict diet coaching. |
| Restaurant explorers | Care about taste fit beyond generic ratings. |
| Solo diners open to optional company | Meal Buddy can add social value without forcing a dating positioning. |
| Early restaurant partners | Can provide menu/dish data and benefit from differentiated discovery. |

## Wedge

The wedge is **personal recommendation from corrected meals**.

A generic review app asks: “What do many people rate highly?”

Haocu asks: “Given what you actually ate, liked, corrected, and need next, what food nearby fits you now?”

## Expansion Path

| Phase | Expansion |
|---|---|
| MVP | Consumer app, AI meal analysis, recommendation, optional Meal Buddy, early restaurant data. |
| Pilot | Closed user group, restaurant partners, Premium/crowdfunding tests. |
| Market Validation | Public campaign, influencer/community launch, restaurant partner package testing. |
| Phase 2 | Restaurant supply-chain / surplus food / ESG workflow if partner-side evidence supports it. |
| Phase 3 | Household nutrition and family meal planning if consumer data and partner infrastructure justify it. |

## Market Claims Boundary

This summary intentionally avoids hard market-size numbers until a sourced market-sizing memo is prepared. Before an investor deck or public campaign uses TAM/SAM/SOM figures, those numbers should be sourced, dated, localized, and reviewed.

## Investor Takeaway

Haocu is pursuing a frequent-use consumer problem with potential restaurant-side monetization and future B2B expansion. The immediate test is not whether every future market is available; it is whether the first user loop can create repeat behavior and differentiated recommendation value.
