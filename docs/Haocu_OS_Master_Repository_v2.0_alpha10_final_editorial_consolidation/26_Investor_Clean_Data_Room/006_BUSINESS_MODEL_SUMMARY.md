## Business Model Principle

Haocu should monetize only in ways that increase utility, trust, or partner value. The free product must still prove the core loop, while Premium and restaurant packages expand depth, limits, identity, and structured discovery.

## Potential Revenue Lines

| Revenue Line | Stage | Summary |
|---|---|---|
| Consumer Premium | MVP / Pilot | Paid plan for higher limits, longer memory, real-person verified social card options, more candidates, and deeper insights. |
| Crowdfunding Support | Pre-launch / Launch | Early access, Premium months, founder supporter badge, mascot goods, and controlled partner bundles. |
| Restaurant Partner Packages | Pilot / Post-MVP | Structured profiles, dish/menu data intake, QR nutrition pages, partner discovery surfaces, and pilot dashboard concepts. |
| Sponsored / Partner Discovery | Later, carefully disclosed | Potential promotion only if clearly labeled and not secretly distorting user trust. |
| B2B / Supply Chain / ESG | Future Phase 2 | Surplus food, demand prediction, and partner workflows after restaurant relationships mature. |
| Household / Family Subscription | Future Phase 3 | Household nutrition planning after core consumer and restaurant loops are validated. |

## Consumer Premium Logic

Premium should not be “pay to make the app usable.” It should be “pay to go deeper.” Potential Premium value:

- more Meal Buddy card capacity
- more candidate recommendations
- longer food diary memory
- saved Top10 insights
- verified real-person identity option
- deeper filters and personalization
- priority access to new restaurant/community features

## Restaurant Value Logic

Restaurant value should come from better fit-based discovery and structured food information, not exaggerated traffic guarantees.

Potential restaurant value:

- better dish-level visibility
- nutrition/ingredient disclosure support
- QR menu/nutrition page
- structured menu intake
- participation in food-intent discovery
- pilot feedback on what users want to eat next

## Crowdfunding Logic

Crowdfunding is best treated as demand validation plus launch funding, not as proof of full-scale company value by itself. It can help test:

- whether the story resonates
- whether Premium months are valuable
- whether mascot/brand assets create shareability
- whether restaurant packages are understandable
- whether community launch channels work

## Model Validation Questions

- Will users pay for deeper memory and better recommendation limits?
- Does real-person verification increase willingness to pay without harming safety?
- Will restaurants pay for structured discovery before the app has large traffic?
- Can crowdfunding rewards be fulfilled without damaging runway?
- Can partner revenue be disclosed clearly enough to preserve trust?

## Clean Investor Framing

The current business model is a staged model: consumer utility first, then Premium/crowdfunding validation, then restaurant partner packages, then future B2B expansion if pilot data supports it.
