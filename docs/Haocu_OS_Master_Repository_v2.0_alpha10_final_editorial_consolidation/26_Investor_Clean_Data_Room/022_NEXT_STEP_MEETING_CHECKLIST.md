# 022 Next-Step Meeting Checklist

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 8.  
> Purpose: Clean investor / advisor / accelerator / restaurant-partner data room.  
> Boundary: External-readable summary only. Not legal, tax, accounting, securities, nutrition, medical, patent, privacy, or investment advice. Sensitive internal drafts and unreviewed details are intentionally excluded.

## Purpose

This checklist helps turn data room interest into productive follow-up meetings with investors, advisors, accelerators, and restaurant partners.

## Before The Meeting

- [ ] Confirm audience type: investor, advisor, accelerator, restaurant, technical reviewer, legal/finance reviewer.
- [ ] Send only the appropriate access level from `020_DOCUMENT_ACCESS_GUIDE.md`.
- [ ] Prepare the 30-second and 3-minute pitch.
- [ ] Prepare demo path or screenshots.
- [ ] Decide what you are asking for: capital, intro, review, pilot, technical feedback, restaurant access, or mentorship.
- [ ] Check that fundraising numbers and legal claims are not presented as final if not reviewed.
- [ ] Prepare 3 specific questions for the reviewer.

## Opening Structure

1. One-line Haocu explanation.
2. Why the problem matters.
3. Show core loop: analyze, correct, save, recommend, optional Meal Buddy.
4. Explain current stage and what has been prepared.
5. Explain next milestone and what help is needed.
6. Ask for critique and next-step fit.

## Questions To Ask Investors

- What evidence would make this investable at the next stage?
- Is the current MVP scope narrow enough?
- Which risk worries you most: AI, social, restaurant adoption, monetization, or compliance?
- Would you prefer to see closed pilot data, crowdfunding traction, or restaurant partner proof first?
- Who else should review this?

## Questions To Ask Advisors

- What should be cut before MVP?
- What do you think is the strongest wedge?
- What would you not say publicly yet?
- What professional review should happen first?
- Would you be willing to review a narrower packet next?

## Questions To Ask Restaurant Partners

- Would you provide structured menu/dish data for a pilot?
- What dish information do customers ask about most?
- What would make a QR nutrition/dish page useful?
- What would make this worth paying for later?
- What claims or workflows would make you uncomfortable?

## After The Meeting

- [ ] Capture objections and red flags.
- [ ] Update investor FAQ if the question repeats.
- [ ] Add accepted feedback to decision log.
- [ ] Mark any legal/finance/privacy claim that needs review.
- [ ] Send a concise follow-up with requested materials.
- [ ] Do not promise equity, compensation, exclusivity, or partnership terms without written review.

## Clean Close

End each serious meeting with one clear next step:

- send demo link
- schedule technical review
- share restaurant packet
- request intro
- confirm pilot discussion
- update after MVP milestone
- ask for investment process and criteria
