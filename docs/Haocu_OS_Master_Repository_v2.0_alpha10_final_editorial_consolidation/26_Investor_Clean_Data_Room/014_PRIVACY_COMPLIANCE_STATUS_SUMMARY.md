# 014 Privacy Compliance Status Summary

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 8.  
> Purpose: Clean investor / advisor / accelerator / restaurant-partner data room.  
> Boundary: External-readable summary only. Not legal, tax, accounting, securities, nutrition, medical, patent, privacy, or investment advice. Sensitive internal drafts and unreviewed details are intentionally excluded.

## Privacy / Compliance Positioning

Haocu handles food, preference, location/context, social, restaurant, and potentially identity-related information. Even when data is not formally medical data, it may feel sensitive to users. The product should treat privacy and safety as trust infrastructure, not as a checkbox.

## Compliance Areas

| Area | Clean Status |
|---|---|
| Privacy policy | Draft requirements exist; final policy requires professional review. |
| Terms of service | Draft requirements exist; final terms require professional review. |
| Nutrition / health claims | Product should remain informational and avoid diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed outcomes. |
| User consent | Consent points should be explicit for profile, meal records, social cards, identity verification, notifications, and partner/sponsored surfaces. |
| Data retention | Retention windows and deletion rights need to be finalized. |
| Social safety | Matching, chat, reporting, blocking, moderation, and identity boundaries need staged implementation. |
| Restaurant disclosure | Partner, sponsored, verified, or nutrition-reviewed labels must be understandable and accurate. |
| AI transparency | AI estimates should show uncertainty/correction paths and not be framed as perfect facts. |

## Product Safety Principles

- Food guidance is not medical advice.
- AI estimates are correctable and should not be treated as definitive.
- User identity exposure should be controlled by plan, consent, and safety design.
- Social features should not force users into unwanted visibility.
- Restaurant partner influence should be disclosed where relevant.
- Demo/staging data should never be confused with production data.

## Clean Investor-Safe Statement

“Haocu is designing privacy, health-claim, AI transparency, and social safety boundaries into the MVP. Final policies and public claims will be reviewed before launch or fundraising publication.”

## Excluded From Clean Data Room

- detailed security implementation notes
- internal vulnerability assumptions
- raw moderation edge-case notes
- unreviewed legal interpretations
- private user data or actual production records
- credentials, tokens, database URLs, or infrastructure secrets

## Review Before Launch

Before public release or campaign launch, complete review for:

- privacy policy
- terms of service
- nutrition/health disclaimer
- AI estimate wording
- social safety and reporting flows
- data retention/deletion process
- restaurant/sponsorship/verification disclosures
- crowdfunding marketing claims

## Investor Takeaway

Haocu’s compliance risk is real but identifiable. The repository already treats privacy, nutrition boundaries, AI uncertainty, social safety, and restaurant disclosure as design requirements rather than afterthoughts.
