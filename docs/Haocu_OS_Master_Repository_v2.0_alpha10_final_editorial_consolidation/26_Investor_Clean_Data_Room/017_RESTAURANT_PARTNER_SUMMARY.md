## Restaurant Partner Thesis

Restaurants are not only advertisers in Haocu. They can become structured food data partners. Better menu and dish information can help users receive more relevant recommendations and help restaurants reach customers whose taste, timing, and nutrition intent better match their food.

## Partner Value Proposition

| Restaurant Need | Haocu Response |
|---|---|
| Customers do not understand dish fit | Structured dish cards can explain ingredients, style, and nutrition-related tags. |
| Public ratings are too generic | Haocu can support personalized fit-based discovery. |
| Menus lack digital structure | Partner intake can organize menu, dish, portion, ingredient, and tag data. |
| Nutrition disclosure is hard | Haocu can provide a reviewed framework for food information presentation. |
| Promotions may feel noisy | Meal-intent discovery can be more relevant than generic ads. |

## MVP Partner Package Concept

A conservative early restaurant pilot may include:

- restaurant profile/card
- selected dish cards
- structured menu intake
- dish tags and nutrition-information draft fields
- QR menu/nutrition page concept
- user feedback collection from pilot flows
- inclusion in demo/pilot recommendation surfaces where appropriate

## What Not To Promise

Do not promise:

- guaranteed foot traffic
- guaranteed ranking
- medical/nutrition certification unless professionally reviewed
- exclusive lead generation
- permanent free promotion
- public claims before restaurant consent
- undisclosed sponsored ranking

## Restaurant Pilot Questions

- Are restaurants willing to provide structured menu/dish data?
- Which fields are easiest and hardest to provide?
- Do restaurants value nutrition-related disclosure or QR pages?
- What pricing model is understandable?
- What partner dashboard would be useful after MVP?
- How should sponsored/partner discovery be labeled?

## Restaurant Partner Talking Point

“Haocu helps users decide what to eat based on their actual taste and meal history. For restaurants, this creates a chance to be discovered by better-fit customers through structured dish data, not just generic ratings.”

## Partner Evidence Needed

- signed or verbal pilot interest
- menu/dish data samples
- restaurant interview notes
- partner pricing feedback
- operational burden estimate
- QR/dish card usefulness feedback
- user actions from restaurant cards

## Clean Boundary

Restaurant commercial terms, pricing, contracts, nutrition review process, and sponsorship disclosures must be reviewed before public launch or paid partner commitments.
