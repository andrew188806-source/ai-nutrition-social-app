## Company Thesis

Haocu / 好廚 is building a Taiwan-first AI food recommendation platform for daily outside eaters. The product starts with an individual utility: help users understand and remember what they eat, then recommend what to eat next based on their actual meals, taste, nutrition pattern, and local restaurant context. Social dining is optional and meal-intent based, not the main reason the app exists.

## One-Line Description

Haocu turns every meal into a private taste and nutrition memory, then uses that memory to recommend what to eat next and, when the user wants, who to eat with.

## Problem

Daily eating decisions are frequent, repetitive, and surprisingly hard to personalize.

People often choose food through habit, generic ratings, vague health goals, influencer posts, or convenience. These signals do not know what the user actually ate recently, what they liked, what they corrected after AI analysis, what restaurants are nearby, or whether they want to eat alone or with others.

Restaurants also face a discovery problem. Public ratings and ads are blunt instruments. A restaurant may be a good fit for a specific user’s taste, budget, health goal, or meal timing, but that fit is rarely represented in traditional restaurant discovery.

## Solution

Haocu builds a feedback loop around real meals:

1. User analyzes or records a meal.
2. AI produces a structured estimate.
3. User can correct the result.
4. The corrected record becomes private food memory.
5. Haocu recommends future meals using intake, preference, and restaurant data.
6. User may optionally create a Meal Buddy card or join a group table around a real meal intention.

## Strategic Positioning

Haocu should be explained as:

- **Food recommendation first**: the main value is better daily food decisions.
- **Nutrition-aware, not medical**: the product can provide food information and lifestyle-oriented guidance, but it is not a medical diagnosis or treatment tool.
- **Social optional**: Meal Buddy and group tables help when users want company, but the app still works for solo users.
- **Restaurant-aligned**: structured restaurant/menu data improves recommendations and creates a potential B2B relationship.
- **Taiwan-first**: Traditional Chinese UX, Taiwan restaurants, Taiwan outside-eating behavior, and local review are the starting point.
