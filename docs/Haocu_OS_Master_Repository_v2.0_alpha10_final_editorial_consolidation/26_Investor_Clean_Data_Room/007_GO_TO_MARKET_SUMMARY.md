# 007 Go-To-Market Summary

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 8.  
> Purpose: Clean investor / advisor / accelerator / restaurant-partner data room.  
> Boundary: External-readable summary only. Not legal, tax, accounting, securities, nutrition, medical, patent, privacy, or investment advice. Sensitive internal drafts and unreviewed details are intentionally excluded.

## GTM Strategy

Haocu should launch through a staged, evidence-driven path rather than a broad paid launch before the product loop is stable.

## GTM Stages

| Stage | Goal | Main Actions | Evidence Needed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Demo Readiness | Make the story tangible | Stabilize 3-minute demo, clean UI, prepare pitch script and screenshots. | Demo completion, clear user flow. |
| 1. Closed Pilot | Test real usage | Invite controlled users, collect meal records, observe corrections and recommendations. | Repeat use, correction behavior, qualitative feedback. |
| 2. Restaurant Pilot | Test partner data | Recruit early restaurants, collect menu/dish data, test dish cards and partner summary. | Partner interest, data intake feasibility. |
| 3. Crowdfunding Prelaunch | Validate demand | Landing page, waitlist, teaser content, mascot/social assets, early supporter messaging. | Email signups, conversion intent, comments/questions. |
| 4. Public Campaign | Test market pull | Launch campaign, influencer/community distribution, early access packages. | Paid support, CAC learning, reward feasibility. |
| 5. Investor Follow-On | Convert evidence into round | Data room update, metrics memo, pilot notes, roadmap, use-of-funds. | Retention, partner evidence, monetization signal. |

## Early Channels

- founder network and warm introductions
- university/startup communities
- food, fitness-light, and lifestyle creators
- restaurant owners and local business groups
- crowdfunding platform prelaunch audience
- demo day / accelerator screens
- social sharing through mascot/food personality content

## Positioning By Audience

| Audience | Message |
|---|---|
| Consumers | “Know what you ate, get better next-meal ideas, and optionally find someone to eat with.” |
| Restaurants | “Turn dish/menu information into more relevant discovery and better customer fit.” |
| Investors | “A frequent food decision loop with personal data memory, optional social action, and restaurant-side expansion.” |
| Accelerators | “A Taiwan-first AI consumer product with clear MVP, backlog, review plan, and testable milestones.” |

## Launch Discipline

GTM should not outpace product trust. Avoid public claims that imply medical advice, guaranteed health outcomes, guaranteed restaurant traffic, guaranteed matching success, or finalized legal/IP status.

## Metrics To Watch

- landing page conversion
- demo/video completion
- closed pilot activation
- saved meals per active user
- correction-to-save rate
- recommendation action rate
- Meal Buddy card creation rate
- restaurant partner response rate
- Premium/crowdfunding conversion intent
- support/refund/complaint themes

## Investor Takeaway

Haocu’s GTM path is designed to generate proof points step by step: first product clarity, then repeated consumer behavior, then restaurant data participation, then crowdfunding demand, then a stronger investor round.
