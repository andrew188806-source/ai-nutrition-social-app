## Finance Principles

- Do not present assumptions as audited facts.
- Tie budget to milestones.
- Separate product development spend from reward fulfillment spend.
- Include legal/compliance review in funding plan.
- Maintain contingency for engineering and review delays.
- Do not give equity to casual contributors or contractors without formal role, vesting, and counsel review.
