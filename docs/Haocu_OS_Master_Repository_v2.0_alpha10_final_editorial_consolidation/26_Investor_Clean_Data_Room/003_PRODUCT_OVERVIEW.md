## Product Summary

Haocu is a mobile-first app that helps users decide what to eat through a loop of AI meal analysis, user correction, private meal memory, next-meal recommendation, and optional social dining.

## Core User Loop

| Step | User Action | Product Value |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Photograph, upload, or record a meal | Reduces friction in food tracking. |
| 2 | Review AI analysis | Gives structured calories, macros, ingredients, and confidence/candidate options. |
| 3 | Correct the meal | Improves record quality and creates trust. |
| 4 | Save to food diary | Builds private taste and nutrition memory. |
| 5 | Receive next-meal recommendation | Converts historical meals into actionable decisions. |
| 6 | Optionally find Meal Buddy / group table | Turns meal intent into low-pressure social action. |

## Key Product Modules

### 1. AI Meal Analysis

- meal photo/upload entry
- candidate meal detection
- user correction path
- estimated calories and nutrition fields
- database-first policy where verified restaurant/menu data exists
- uncertainty and correction loop rather than pretending AI is always right

### 2. Food Diary / Today Intake

- daily meal records
- nutrition summary
- saved corrected meals
- history window and Premium memory depth
- meal rating and feedback logic

### 3. Recommendation

- next-meal suggestions based on recent intake, taste memory, restaurant context, and user preference
- not dependent only on public ratings
- designed to become more personalized as corrected data accumulates

### 4. Meal Buddy

- meal-intent card creation
- anonymous/free and verified/Premium identity paths
- candidate list limits by plan
- invitation/chat flows
- clear separation between food utility and social function

### 5. Group Tables

- multi-person meal table concept
- controlled invitation/joining flow
- group chat boundary
- Premium-led table expansion in later stages

### 6. Restaurant Surface

- restaurant and dish cards
- dish-based Meal Buddy card creation
- structured menu and nutrition data intake
- restaurant partner summary and future dashboard path

### 7. Admin / Review Layer

- restaurant verification concepts
- nutrition disclosure review
- moderation and review queues
- audit and compliance-oriented data handling

## Product Differentiation

| Existing Alternative | Limitation | Haocu Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Restaurant review apps | Public average ratings are not personal. | Recommendations can use the user’s own corrected meals and taste memory. |
| Calorie trackers | Often feel manual, isolated, and non-social. | Haocu converts tracking into next-meal decisions and optional meal matching. |
| Social/dating apps | Social intent is broad and often high-pressure. | Haocu starts from a real meal intention and can remain anonymous/free by default. |
| Restaurant ads | Ads may not match user fit. | Restaurant data can support fit-based discovery with disclosure rules. |

## Product Tone

The app should feel clean, warm, useful, and demo-friendly. It should not overwhelm users with dense feature surfaces. The product should prioritize a simple path: analyze food, understand result, save meal, get recommendation, optionally take a social or restaurant action.

## Product Boundary

The MVP should not attempt to deliver every future vision at once. Household nutrition, surplus food supply chain, deeper B2B analytics, and multi-photo capture expansions are future opportunities, not first-launch requirements.
