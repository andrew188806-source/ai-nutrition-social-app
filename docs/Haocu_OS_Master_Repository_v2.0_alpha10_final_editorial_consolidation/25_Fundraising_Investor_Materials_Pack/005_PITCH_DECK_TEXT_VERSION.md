## Slide 1 — Title

**Haocu / 好廚**  
AI food recommendation and optional social dining for Taiwan outside eaters.

Tagline: **What you ate. What you like. What to eat next. Who to eat with.**

## Slide 2 — Problem

Daily food decisions are high-frequency but poorly personalized.

- Generic ratings do not know individual taste.
- Calorie apps are often manual and punitive.
- Restaurant information is fragmented.
- Social dining apps start from profiles, not meal intent.
- Users want practical help, not another complicated health dashboard.

## Slide 3 — Insight

A meal is more than a photo.

A corrected meal record contains taste, timing, location, nutrition pattern, restaurant context, and social intent. Over time, this becomes a private food memory that can power better recommendations than generic public ratings.

## Slide 4 — Solution

Haocu turns meals into personalized recommendations.

- Analyze or record meals.
- Let users correct AI results.
- Build private taste and nutrition memory.
- Recommend next meal and restaurant.
- Let users optionally create Meal Buddy cards or group tables.

## Slide 5 — Product Demo Flow

1. AI meal analysis.
2. Candidate selection and correction.
3. Today Intake / Food Diary update.
4. Next-meal recommendation.
5. Restaurant card and dish CTA.
6. Meal Buddy card creation.
7. Candidate invite / chat / group table.

## Slide 6 — User Value

For users:

- Less decision fatigue.
- Better food discovery.
- More personal than public ratings.
- Nutrition awareness without shame.
- Optional social eating when they want it.

## Slide 7 — Restaurant Value

For restaurants:

- Structured dish and nutrition presence.
- More relevant discovery.
- Food-intent based traffic.
- QR nutrition pages and menu data workflows.
- Early partner positioning in a local food intelligence graph.

## Slide 8 — Differentiation

| Category | Typical Approach | Haocu Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Food recognition | One-off AI guess | AI + user correction + memory |
| Review platforms | Crowd average | User-specific taste fit |
| Calorie tracking | Manual logging | AI-assisted informational summary |
| Social apps | Profile first | Meal intent first |
| Restaurant ads | Sponsored visibility | Transparent partner/data layer |

## Slide 9 — Business Model

- Consumer Premium: limits, memory, Top10, verified real-person card, deeper recommendations.
- Restaurant partner packages: structured menu/nutrition data, QR pages, reviewed profile, pilot packages.
- Crowdfunding: early access, Premium bundles, mascot goods, restaurant packages.
- Future: supply-chain/ESG and household nutrition expansions.
