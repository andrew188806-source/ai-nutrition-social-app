## Core Story

Haocu begins with a small daily pain: deciding what to eat. This pain is frequent, personal, and connected to health, taste, time, money, location, and social mood. Existing tools solve parts of the problem, but no single product builds a private memory of what the user actually eats and uses it to recommend the next meal.

Haocu’s wedge is meal analysis and correction. The user takes a photo or records a meal, Haocu estimates the food and nutrition, and the user corrects it. The corrected record is the key asset: it is more trustworthy than a raw AI guess and more personal than a public review.

From there, the product expands naturally:

- food diary and nutrition awareness
- next-meal recommendation
- restaurant discovery
- optional Meal Buddy or group table
- restaurant structured data partnerships

## Investor Framing

Haocu should be framed as a **personal food intelligence platform**, not a simple calorie tracker or social app.

The founder should emphasize:

1. High-frequency behavior: eating decisions happen daily.
2. Data compounding: correction improves memory and personalization.
3. Local wedge: Taiwan outside-eating and restaurant density create a strong first market.
4. Optional social: meal intent gives social features practical context.
5. B2B expansion: restaurant menu data improves recommendation quality and creates business-side value.

## Why This Can Be Big

Haocu can start with one practical decision and expand into a broader food operating system:

- consumer food memory
- restaurant data graph
- personalized discovery
- group dining behavior
- structured menu/nutrition infrastructure
- future household and supply-chain integrations

However, investors should see staged discipline. The MVP does not need to prove the entire future. It needs to prove repeated use, recommendation value, and willingness to pay.

## What Not To Say

Avoid:

- “We are a medical nutrition app.”
- “AI will perfectly recognize every food.”
- “We will replace Google Maps or delivery apps.”
- “Restaurants will definitely pay before pilot proof.”
- “Social matching is safe automatically.”
- “Future supply-chain phases are already guaranteed.”

## What To Say Instead

Say:

- “We provide food information and recommendation, not medical advice.”
- “Correction is built into the AI loop.”
- “We focus on personal fit, not replacing all discovery tools.”
- “Restaurant pricing will be validated through pilots.”
- “Safety and consent are core product boundaries.”
- “Future phases are staged after MVP validation.”
