# 017 Risk And Mitigation Summary

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 7C.  
> Purpose: Fundraising / investor / accelerator / partner communication package.  
> Boundary: Draft material only. All valuation, securities, legal, tax, nutrition, patent, privacy, and financial claims require professional review before external publication.

## Investor-Ready Risk Framing

Do not hide risks. Show that the repository already contains engineering, review, and business mitigation paths.

## Risk Matrix

| Risk | Investor Concern | Mitigation | Related Pack |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI accuracy | Users may lose trust if food recognition is wrong | Candidate selection, correction flow, database-first AI, uncertainty display | 03_AI, 23_Engineering |
| Nutrition/health claims | Regulatory or trust risk | Informational positioning, no diagnosis/treatment claims, professional review | 14_Compliance, 24_Review |
| Social safety | Harassment, identity misuse, unsafe meetings | anonymous free cards, verification, reporting, moderation, consent flows | 02_PRD, 13_Security |
| Restaurant adoption | B2B value may be unproven | pilot-first packages, structured intake, partner feedback | 16_Business |
| Monetization | Premium or restaurant revenue may not convert | willingness-to-pay tests, crowdfunding validation, pricing experiments | 18_Finance |
| Engineering complexity | Too many modules for MVP | Alpha 7A P0/P1/P2 backlog and sprint plan | 23_Engineering |
| Data privacy | Food/social data is sensitive in context | RLS, privacy review, retention policy, consent model | 04_Data, 14_Compliance |
| Crowdfunding execution | Reward costs and claims may create obligations | cost model, legal review, conservative packages | 24_Review |
| IP defensibility | Features may be copyable | trade secrets, corrected data loop, brand, patent review queue | 17_Legal_IP |
| Founder bandwidth | Founder may be pulled into too many tracks | focus on MVP loop, advisor structure, issue backlog | 23_Engineering |

## Red Flags Before External Launch

- No privacy policy / terms review.
- Medical-sounding nutrition claims.
- No reporting/moderation path for social features.
- Restaurant partner claims guarantee traffic.
- Crowdfunding rewards priced without fulfillment cost.
- Demo crashes during core path.
- No single source of truth for meal records.

## Risk Narrative For Investors

“The risks are real, but they are identifiable and staged. We are not asking investors to fund a vague food super-app. We are funding a focused MVP loop with professional review and pilot validation built into the plan.”
