# 012 Business Model Summary

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 7C.  
> Purpose: Fundraising / investor / accelerator / partner communication package.  
> Boundary: Draft material only. All valuation, securities, legal, tax, nutrition, patent, privacy, and financial claims require professional review before external publication.

## Revenue Principles

- Monetization should improve trust and utility.
- Free users must experience the core loop.
- Premium should unlock depth, limits, identity options, and memory.
- Restaurant revenue must not secretly distort recommendations.
- Crowdfunding should validate demand without overpromising delivery.

## Consumer Premium

Potential Premium features:

- higher daily Meal Buddy card limits
- more candidate recommendations
- longer food diary memory
- Top10 saved food insights
- verified real-person card
- deeper recommendation filters
- advanced taste/nutrition summaries
- priority access to restaurant/community features

## Restaurant Revenue

Potential restaurant packages:

- structured restaurant profile
- dish/menu data intake
- nutrition/ingredient disclosure pages
- QR menu/nutrition page
- reviewed or verified data status
- restaurant pilot dashboard
- food-intent discovery participation

## Crowdfunding

Potential supporter packages:

- early access
- Premium months
- founder supporter badge
- mascot stickers/cards/acrylic goods
- restaurant coupon bundles if legally/commercially reviewed
- restaurant partner launch package

## Future Revenue Streams

Not MVP promises:

- B2B supply-chain / surplus food tools
- community group dining events
- household nutrition planning
- family subscription
- restaurant analytics

## Validation Required

Before locking pricing:

- interview users on Premium value
- test preorder / crowdfunding conversion
- test restaurant willingness to pay
- calculate reward cost and delivery burden
- review taxes/accounting/crowdfunding obligations
- review consumer protection and advertising rules

## Business Model Acceptance Criteria

- Users understand what is free and what is paid.
- Recommendations disclose sponsored/partner influence where relevant.
- Restaurant packages avoid guaranteed traffic claims.
- Premium does not block the basic value loop.
- Pricing assumptions are marked as assumptions until validated.
