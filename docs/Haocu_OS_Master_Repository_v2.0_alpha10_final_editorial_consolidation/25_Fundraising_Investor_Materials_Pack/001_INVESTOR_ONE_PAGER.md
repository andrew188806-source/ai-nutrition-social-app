## Company

**Haocu / 好廚** is building a Taiwan-first AI food recommendation platform for daily outside eaters, with optional social dining and restaurant data partnerships.

## One-Line Pitch

Haocu turns every meal into a private taste and nutrition memory, then uses that memory to recommend what to eat next and, when the user wants, who to eat with.

## Problem

Daily food decisions are repetitive, fragmented, and poorly personalized.

People rely on generic reviews, calorie guesses, social media posts, and habit. These inputs do not know the user's actual taste, nutrition pattern, recent meals, location, social comfort, or meal intent. Restaurant discovery is also noisy: high public ratings do not always mean the food fits this specific user.

## Solution

Haocu starts with a simple loop:

1. Analyze or record a meal.
2. Let the user correct the AI result.
3. Save the corrected record into a private food diary.
4. Recommend the next meal using recent intake, taste preference, and restaurant data.
5. Optionally create a Meal Buddy card or group table when the user wants company.

## Why Now

AI photo analysis, consumer willingness to use food apps, restaurant digitization, and social fatigue create an opening for a product that is useful first and social second. Haocu does not ask users to post everything publicly. It builds personal food intelligence privately, then gives optional social actions only when useful.

## Product

MVP modules:

- Mobile app: AI meal analysis, correction, meal diary, nutrition summary, recommendation, Meal Buddy, group table, chat/invite, Premium limits.
- Restaurant surface: restaurant cards, dish cards, nutrition tags, Meal Buddy card creation from restaurant dish.
- Restaurant/admin tools: structured menu and nutrition intake, review status, partner onboarding.
- Data layer: meal records, corrected AI analysis, restaurant/menu graph, social card, match/chat/table state.

## Differentiation

| Existing Behavior | Haocu Difference |
|---|---|
| Generic public ratings | Personal recommendations based on the user's own corrected meals and taste memory. |
| Calorie tracking feels punitive | Nutrition summary is informational, not shame-based or medicalized. |
| Social apps start with profile swiping | Haocu starts with real meal intent: what to eat, where, when, and with whom. |
| Restaurant ads can feel hidden | Recommendation and sponsorship rules must remain transparent and reviewed. |
| AI guesses can be wrong | User correction is part of the product loop, not an exception. |

## Business Model

Planned revenue streams:

- Consumer Premium: higher Meal Buddy limits, longer memory, Top10 food diary, real-person card after verification, deeper recommendations.
- Restaurant tools and partner packages: structured menu/nutrition pages, QR pages, discovery participation, pilot packages.
- Crowdfunding and early supporter packages: Premium bundles, mascot goods, restaurant partner packages.
- Future phases: supply-chain/ESG and household nutrition products are roadmap items, not MVP promises.

## Go-To-Market

1. Demo-ready MVP for founder-led demos.
2. Closed pilot with founder network, outside eaters, students/offices, and early food communities.
3. Restaurant pilot with menu-friendly restaurants such as healthy lunch boxes, cafes, light meals, and casual restaurants.
4. Crowdfunding to validate consumer demand and brand pull.
5. Public beta with focused Taiwan city/district launch.

## Key Risks

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| AI accuracy | Database-first AI, candidate selection, correction flow, uncertainty display. |
| Social safety | Anonymous free cards, verification for real-person cards, moderation/reporting, consent boundaries. |
| Health claims | Food information only; no diagnosis, treatment, or guaranteed health outcomes. |
| Monetization | Validate Premium willingness to pay and restaurant partner value before scaling. |
| Restaurant data quality | Structured intake, review workflow, verified status, correction loops. |
