## Market Thesis

Haocu targets a high-frequency behavior: daily food choice among outside eaters. The opportunity is not only food logging or restaurant discovery. It is the intersection of:

- AI-assisted meal understanding
- personal taste memory
- nutrition awareness
- local restaurant data
- optional real-world social dining
- consumer Premium and restaurant-side tools

## Initial Market

Taiwan-first, district-focused launch.

Primary early users:

- office workers who eat out frequently
- students and young professionals
- people casually managing nutrition without strict dieting
- people who enjoy food discovery but distrust generic ratings
- people open to occasional food-based social interaction

## Why Taiwan Is A Good Starting Point

- Dense local restaurant options make discovery hard but useful.
- Outside eating is culturally common.
- Restaurant menus are varied and often local-context-heavy.
- District-by-district pilots are practical.
- Founder-led restaurant onboarding can generate structured local data.

## Market Segments

| Segment | Pain | Haocu Value |
|---|---|---|
| Outside eaters | Decision fatigue and repeated meals | Next-meal recommendation and food memory |
| Casual health users | Want awareness without strict dieting | Informational nutrition summary |
| Food explorers | Generic ratings are not personal | Taste-fit recommendation |
| Social diners | Want company but dislike cold social matching | Meal-intent based Meal Buddy cards |
| Restaurants | Need relevant discovery and clearer menu data | Structured dish pages and partner flow |

## Validation Metrics Before Market-Size Claims

Do not rely only on top-down market-size estimates. Collect bottom-up evidence:

- weekly active meal recorders
- analysis-to-save conversion
- correction completion rate
- recommendation click-through / acceptance
- restaurant card engagement
- Meal Buddy card creation rate
- invite-to-chat or invite-to-eat conversion
- Premium intent survey / preorder
- restaurant pilot signup and data completion
- crowdfunding conversion

## Expandability

If the MVP works, Haocu can expand from consumer recommendation into:

- restaurant menu intelligence
- sponsored but transparent dish discovery
- food diary insights
- group dining and community events
- supply-chain/ESG pilots
- household nutrition planning
