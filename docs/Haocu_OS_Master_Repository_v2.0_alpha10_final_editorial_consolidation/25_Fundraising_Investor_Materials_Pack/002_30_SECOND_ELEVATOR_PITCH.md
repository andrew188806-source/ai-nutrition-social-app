# 002 30-Second Elevator Pitch

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 7C.  
> Purpose: Fundraising / investor / accelerator / partner communication package.  
> Boundary: Draft material only. All valuation, securities, legal, tax, nutrition, patent, privacy, and financial claims require professional review before external publication.

## Version A — Investor

Haocu is a Taiwan-first AI food recommendation app for daily outside eaters. Users analyze or record meals, correct the AI result, and Haocu builds a private taste and nutrition memory to recommend what to eat next. The social layer is optional: users can turn a meal intent into a Meal Buddy card or group table. We are starting with MVP validation, restaurant menu data, and a Premium model, then using crowdfunding and pilots to prove demand.

## Version B — Restaurant Partner

Haocu helps restaurants become easier to discover by the right customers. Instead of only relying on generic ratings, users get recommendations based on their taste, recent meals, and nutrition context. Restaurants can provide structured menu and nutrition information, show clearer dish cards, and participate in food-intent discovery through Meal Buddy and group dining flows.

## Version C — Consumer

每天外食不知道吃什麼，又怕踩雷？Haocu 會記住你真正吃過、修正過、喜歡過的餐點，幫你推薦下一餐。想自己吃可以，想找飯友也可以。它不是逼你節食，而是讓你更懂今天吃了什麼、下餐怎麼選比較適合自己。

## Version D — Accelerator

Haocu combines AI meal analysis, private taste memory, restaurant data, and optional food-based social matching into one Taiwan-first MVP. The repository is already organized into engineering backlog, professional review pack, and fundraising materials, so the next milestone is execution: demo stabilization, closed pilot, restaurant onboarding, and crowdfunding validation.

## Delivery Notes

- Do not open with “social app.” Open with daily food decision pain.
- Social dining is an expansion of food intent, not the core identity.
- Avoid medical claims; say food information and personalized recommendation.
- Avoid saying AI is always correct; say correction is part of the loop.
