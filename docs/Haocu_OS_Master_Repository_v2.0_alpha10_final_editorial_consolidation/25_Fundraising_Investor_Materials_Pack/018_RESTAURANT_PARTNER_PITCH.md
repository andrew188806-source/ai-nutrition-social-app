## Restaurant One-Liner

Haocu helps restaurants turn menu data into more relevant discovery: users find dishes that fit their taste, recent meals, and nutrition context, instead of relying only on generic ratings.

## Restaurant Problem

Restaurants often rely on:

- generic ratings
- delivery platform visibility
- social media posts
- paid ads
- static menus

But these channels do not always explain who the food is best for or why a user should choose it today.

## Haocu Value

Haocu can help restaurants:

- present structured dish information
- show nutrition/ingredient context where appropriate
- appear in personalized recommendations
- support QR nutrition/menu pages
- connect users to Meal Buddy cards around specific dishes
- collect feedback for dish-level discovery

## Pilot Offer

A first restaurant pilot should include:

- restaurant profile setup
- menu/dish intake
- dish card formatting
- nutrition estimate/review status
- QR page concept
- user recommendation test
- partner feedback session

## Restaurant Script

We are building Haocu, an AI food recommendation app for people who eat out often. Instead of only showing restaurants by public rating, Haocu learns what users actually eat and like, then recommends dishes and restaurants that fit them.

For restaurants, we are starting with a pilot: we help structure your menu and dish information so your food can be recommended more clearly. This is not a guaranteed-traffic ad product. It is an early data and discovery partnership.

## Restaurant Objections

### “Will this guarantee more customers?”

No. We should not promise guaranteed traffic. The pilot goal is to test whether structured menu data improves discovery and user interest.

### “Is this a nutrition certification?”

No. Nutrition data must be presented carefully with review status and disclaimers. It is not medical certification.

### “How much work do restaurants need to do?”

The first pilot should minimize work: menu photos/files, dish names, ingredients if available, prices, portion notes, and optional dish photos.

### “Why should we join early?”

Early restaurants can shape the data format, become reference partners, and be part of the first local food recommendation graph.

## Pilot Success Metrics

- menu data completion
- dish card completion
- QR page readiness
- user engagement with restaurant card
- restaurant feedback score
- willingness to continue after pilot
