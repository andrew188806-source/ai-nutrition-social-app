## Company Description

Haocu is a Taiwan-first AI food recommendation and optional social dining platform for people who eat out frequently. Users analyze or record meals, correct AI outputs, build private taste and nutrition memory, receive next-meal recommendations, and optionally find Meal Buddies or group tables around real food intent. Restaurants can provide structured menu and dish data to improve discovery.

## Problem

Daily food decisions are high-frequency but poorly personalized. Generic restaurant ratings do not understand individual taste or recent nutrition context. Calorie apps can be high-friction and shame-based. Social apps rarely start from a real-world meal plan. Restaurants lack structured ways to connect dish-level information to the right users.

## Solution

Haocu starts with the meal. AI analysis reduces logging friction; user correction improves trust and data quality. Corrected meal records power personal recommendations and optional social dining. Structured restaurant data improves local food discovery.

## Why Now

AI can reduce meal logging friction, but raw recognition is not enough. The opportunity is to combine AI assistance with correction, private memory, local restaurant data, and real-world meal intent.

## Target Market

Initial target: Taiwan outside eaters, especially office workers, students, young professionals, casual nutrition-aware users, food explorers, and users open to optional meal companionship.

## Business Model

Consumer Premium, restaurant partner packages, crowdfunding/early supporter packages, and future staged expansions into supply-chain/ESG and household nutrition.
