## Script

### 1. Hook

Every day, millions of people eat out and face the same small but recurring decision: **What should I eat next?** The answer should depend on taste, recent meals, nutrition context, location, time, budget, social mood, and trust. But current tools handle only pieces of that decision.

### 2. Problem

Generic ratings tell us what the crowd liked. They do not know whether I like sweet sauces, oily food, spicy dishes, light meals, or high-protein lunches. Calorie apps track numbers, but often require heavy manual input and can feel punitive. Social apps focus on profiles, not shared meal intent. Restaurants want visibility, but ads and public rankings do not always connect them to the right diners.

### 3. Solution

Haocu creates a meal-centered recommendation loop.

The user analyzes or records a meal. AI estimates dish and nutrition, then the user corrects it. That correction becomes a better memory than a raw AI guess. Over time, Haocu builds private taste memory, nutrition context, food diary, and restaurant preference.

From there, Haocu can recommend the next meal and restaurant more personally. Then, if the user wants company, the same food intent becomes a Meal Buddy card or group table.

### 4. Product Demo Walkthrough

The MVP demo should show:

1. User opens AI analysis.
2. User captures or uploads meal photo.
3. AI shows candidate dishes and nutrition estimates.
4. User confirms or corrects the result.
5. Meal is saved to Today Intake and Food Diary.
6. Next-meal recommendation updates.
7. User creates a Meal Buddy card from the meal or restaurant dish.
8. User sees candidates, invitation state, and chat/group flow.
9. Restaurant card shows structured dish information and partner value.

The key demo principle is clarity: one reliable 3-minute path is better than showing every feature.

### 5. Differentiation

Haocu is different because it combines four layers:

- **AI analysis with correction**: not just one-time image recognition.
- **Private taste and nutrition memory**: recommendations are based on the user’s actual history.
- **Local restaurant/menu graph**: restaurant data improves accuracy and discovery.
- **Optional social intent**: social matching starts from real meal context, not profile browsing.

### 6. Business Model

Initial revenue streams:

- Consumer Premium: more Meal Buddy creation, more candidates, longer memory, Top10, verified real-person cards, deeper recommendation features.
- Restaurant partner packages: structured menu data, nutrition QR pages, reviewed profile, pilot participation, potential discovery tools.
- Crowdfunding: early access, Premium bundles, mascot goods, restaurant packages, and supporter community.

Future roadmap phases include supply-chain/ESG and household nutrition, but these remain staged expansion opportunities.

### 7. GTM

The launch sequence is intentionally narrow:

- Stabilize MVP demo.
- Run closed pilot with outside eaters.
- Onboard early restaurants with structured menus.
- Validate repeated meal logging, recommendation usefulness, social comfort, and Premium willingness to pay.
- Use crowdfunding to validate market pull and brand community.
- Expand by city/district after the first loop works.

### 8. Defensibility

The defensible asset is not “AI can recognize food.” That will become common. The asset is the combination of corrected food records, taste memory, restaurant menu data, social meal intent, and trust boundaries.

Each correction makes the user profile more useful. Each restaurant menu makes the local graph better. Each social interaction adds intent data that generic ratings do not have.

### 9. Risk and Mitigation

The main risks are known:

- AI may be wrong, so correction is built into the loop.
- Nutrition claims can be risky, so Haocu stays informational and avoids medical claims.
- Social features require safety, so identity, consent, reporting, and verification are part of the design.
- Restaurant adoption must be validated, so the first partner offer is a pilot, not a scaled sales promise.
- Monetization must be tested, so Premium and restaurant pricing remain assumptions until pilot data exists.

### 10. Ask

The immediate goal is to fund engineering execution, closed pilot, restaurant onboarding, professional review, and crowdfunding readiness. We are looking for investors and advisors who can help us move from repository and demo to real MVP validation.

## Final Line

Haocu’s wedge is simple: help people decide what to eat next. The long-term platform is larger: a personal food operating system connecting meals, health context, taste, restaurants, and optional real-world dining.
