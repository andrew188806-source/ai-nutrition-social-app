# 006 Demo Day Script

> Repository Status: Haocu OS Master Repository v2.0 Alpha 7C.  
> Purpose: Fundraising / investor / accelerator / partner communication package.  
> Boundary: Draft material only. All valuation, securities, legal, tax, nutrition, patent, privacy, and financial claims require professional review before external publication.

## Demo Goal

Show that Haocu is not just a concept: it has a clear user loop, engineering plan, professional-review path, and fundable milestone plan.

## 5-Minute Demo Day Flow

### 0:00–0:30 — Hook

Every day outside eaters make the same decision: what should I eat next? Today that decision is split across generic ratings, memory, random cravings, and incomplete nutrition guesses. Haocu turns every meal into a private taste and nutrition memory, then recommends what to eat next.

### 0:30–1:20 — Product Loop

Show AI analysis:

- photo or upload
- AI candidate result
- nutrition estimate
- user correction
- save to Today Intake

Narration: The correction is important. Haocu does not depend on pretending AI is perfect. The correction becomes better data.

### 1:20–2:00 — Recommendation

Show Today Intake and next-meal recommendation.

Narration: Once Haocu knows what you actually ate, it can recommend the next meal with context, not just popularity.

### 2:00–2:45 — Optional Social

Show Meal Buddy card creation and candidate list.

Narration: Social is optional and meal-intent based. Users can stay anonymous for free or verify for real-person cards on Premium.

### 2:45–3:20 — Restaurant Value

Show restaurant card and dish CTA.

Narration: Restaurants can provide structured menu and nutrition data, making their dishes easier to match with the right users.

### 3:20–4:10 — Business and GTM

Narration:

The first business model is consumer Premium, restaurant partner packages, and crowdfunding. The first GTM path is demo stabilization, closed pilot, restaurant onboarding, crowdfunding validation, then district-focused public beta.

### 4:10–4:45 — Moat

Narration:

Food recognition itself will commoditize. The defensibility is the corrected meal memory, private taste graph, restaurant menu graph, and food-intent social layer.

### 4:45–5:00 — Ask

We are looking for investors, advisors, and partners to help move from repository and demo to MVP validation: engineering execution, restaurant pilots, professional review, and crowdfunding readiness.

## Demo Reliability Rules

- Do not show unstable paths.
- Use one clean demo account.
- Use one prepared restaurant and one prepared meal.
- Keep all UI clean and spacious.
- Do not over-explain technical architecture unless asked.
