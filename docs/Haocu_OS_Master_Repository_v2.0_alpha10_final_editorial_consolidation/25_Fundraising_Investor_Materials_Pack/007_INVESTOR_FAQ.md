## Product Questions

### What is Haocu?

Haocu is a Taiwan-first AI food recommendation platform for outside eaters. It uses meal analysis, user correction, private taste/nutrition memory, restaurant data, and optional social dining to recommend what to eat next.

### Is this a calorie tracker?

No. Haocu includes nutrition information, but the core product is food recommendation and food memory. It should avoid shame-based dieting and medical claims.

### Is this a dating or social app?

No. Social dining is optional. The product starts from meal intent: what to eat, where, when, and whether the user wants company.

### What is the MVP?

The MVP includes AI meal analysis, correction, meal diary, next-meal recommendation, restaurant cards, Meal Buddy cards, invitation/chat flows, group table demo, Premium limits, and early restaurant/admin data workflows.

## AI Questions

### What if AI food recognition is wrong?

Haocu assumes AI can be wrong. Candidate selection and user correction are part of the product loop. Corrected records are more valuable than unverified AI guesses.

### What makes the AI defensible?

The defensible layer is not generic image recognition. It is corrected meal records, user taste memory, restaurant/menu graph, feedback loops, and food-intent context.

### Does Haocu provide medical advice?

No. Haocu should provide food information and general meal context only. It should not diagnose, treat, prescribe, or guarantee health outcomes.

## Market Questions

### Why start in Taiwan?

Taiwan has dense outside-eating behavior, high restaurant variety, strong convenience culture, and local food contexts that generic global apps do not handle deeply. Taiwan is also a practical market for founder-led restaurant pilots and district-by-district launch.

### What is the first target segment?

Outside eaters who repeatedly face meal decisions: office workers, students, people managing nutrition casually, food explorers, and users who may want occasional meal companionship.

### How will you validate demand?

Through closed pilot retention, meal-analysis completion, correction rate, recommendation click/usefulness feedback, Meal Buddy creation, Premium interest, restaurant pilot feedback, and crowdfunding conversion.

## Business Questions

### How will Haocu make money?

Initial streams: consumer Premium, restaurant partner packages, crowdfunding/early supporter packages. Future phases may add supply-chain/ESG and household nutrition models, but MVP monetization should be validated first.

### What is Premium?

Premium may include higher Meal Buddy limits, more candidate recommendations, longer diary memory, Top10 saved insights, real-person card after verification, and deeper recommendation/personalization features.

### Why would restaurants pay?

Restaurants may value structured menu visibility, nutrition/discovery pages, QR pages, early partner positioning, food-intent traffic, and better match to users whose preferences fit their dishes. This must be validated in pilot.

## Fundraising Questions

The amount should be inserted after finance/securities review. The recommended structure is milestone-based: enough to stabilize MVP, run pilot, onboard restaurants, complete professional review, and prepare crowdfunding or the next investment round.

### What will funds be used for?

Engineering, Supabase/backend, AI analysis/recommendation, design/QA, restaurant onboarding, legal/IP/privacy review, crowdfunding preparation, and initial growth testing.

### What is the next fundable milestone?

A stable MVP demo, closed pilot metrics, restaurant pilot evidence, reviewed legal/privacy/health-claims boundaries, and crowdfunding-ready materials.

## Risk Questions

### What are the biggest risks?

AI accuracy, social safety, health-claim boundaries, monetization validation, restaurant data quality, engineering execution, and fundraising/legal compliance.

### How are these risks mitigated?

Alpha 7A provides engineering backlog. Alpha 7B provides professional review queue. Alpha 7C provides fundraising narrative. Product-level mitigations include correction loops, database-first policy, social verification/reporting, and staged pilot launch.

## Closing Answer

Haocu is fundable if it proves repeated use and trust: users save/correct meals, recommendations become useful, restaurants provide data, and optional social dining increases engagement without harming safety.
