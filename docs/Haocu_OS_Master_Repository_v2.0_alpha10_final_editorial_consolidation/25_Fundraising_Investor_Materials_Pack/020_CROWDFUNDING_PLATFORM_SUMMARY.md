## Campaign Positioning

Haocu is a personal food recommendation app for people who eat out often. It helps users understand what they ate, remember what they like, discover what to eat next, and optionally find Meal Buddies.

## Public Story

每天外食，最難的不是吃飯，是不知道下一餐怎麼選。  
Haocu 想做一個越吃越懂你的 App：不是只看大眾評分，也不是逼你節食，而是把你真的吃過、修正過、喜歡過的餐點變成自己的飲食記憶。

## Campaign Promise Boundary

The campaign should promise early access and MVP development, not medical outcomes or perfect AI.

Do not promise:

- guaranteed weight loss
- medical nutrition treatment
- perfect food recognition
- guaranteed restaurant discounts
- guaranteed matching or safe offline meetings
- complete nationwide restaurant coverage at launch

## Reward Concepts

| Tier | Concept | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Supporter | thank-you badge / founder wall | low fulfillment burden |
| Early User | early access + Premium months | must define delivery date |
| Food Memory Pack | Premium + mascot digital goods | good brand fit |
| Mascot Goods | sticker/card/acrylic/scale card | cost and logistics review required |
| Social Explorer | Premium + Meal Buddy perks | must fit privacy/safety review |
| Restaurant Partner | pilot listing / menu data setup | must avoid guaranteed traffic claims |
