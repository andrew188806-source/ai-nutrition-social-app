## Clean-Sharing Principle

Alpha 8 does not delete or replace the internal repository. It adds a controlled external layer. Sensitive internal drafts, unreviewed legal/patent details, private finance assumptions, raw security details, and unresolved founder brainstorming should remain internal unless specifically reviewed and shared under the right context.
