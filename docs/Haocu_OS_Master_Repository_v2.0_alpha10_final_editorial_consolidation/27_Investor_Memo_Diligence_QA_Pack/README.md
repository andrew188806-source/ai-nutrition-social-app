## What This Folder Adds

Alpha 8 made the repository externally readable. Alpha 9 makes it usable in an actual fundraising process.

This pack is designed for the moment after an investor, advisor, accelerator, restaurant partner, or platform reviewer says:

> "Send me more details," "Can we diligence this?", "Do you have evidence?", "What changed since the last meeting?", or "How do we track next steps?"

It provides:

- a formal investor memo that can be sent before or after a first serious meeting
- short memo variants for warm intros and quick angel review
- master diligence Q&A organized by investor concern
- a live diligence request list and evidence map
- meeting agenda, notes, follow-up, and response tracking templates
- demo evidence and pilot evidence capture frameworks
- objection handling and red-flag response bank
- update templates for investors, advisors, and potential board observers

## Evidence Discipline

Every claim shared with investors should be tagged as one of the following:

| Evidence Level | Meaning | Investor-Safe Use |
|---|---|---|
| Planned | Product or process is specified but not yet built. | Use as roadmap only. |
| Demo | Present in prototype, mock, demo build, or scripted walkthrough. | Say "demoed" or "prototype demonstrates". |
| Pilot | Tested with a restaurant, user, small group, or operational workflow. | Say "pilot evidence" and provide scope. |
| Live | Used in production by real users or restaurants. | Say "live" only when true. |
| Verified | Confirmed by third-party reviewer, analytics export, signed document, or professional review. | Strong diligence evidence. |

Do not inflate the evidence level. A clean, honest "planned / demo / pilot" answer is stronger than an unsupported live-traction claim.
