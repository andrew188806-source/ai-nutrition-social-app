# Restaurant Pilot Q&A

> Use: Answers for restaurants and investors asking how restaurant pilots work.

## Q1. What does a restaurant need to do in the pilot?
A restaurant pilot can start lightweight: provide menu items, photos if available, portion/ingredient notes where possible, and permission to create a Haocu restaurant card or nutrition/info page for testing.

## Q2. Does the restaurant need full nutrition data?
Not at the beginning. Haocu can start with structured menu fields and AI-assisted estimates, but any public nutrition claim should be carefully labeled and reviewed. The strongest long-term path is combining restaurant-provided data with AI estimation and user feedback.

## Q3. What does the restaurant get?
Potential pilot benefits include a cleaner restaurant profile, menu/nutrition visibility, early access to food recommendation flows, customer insight, and possible traffic from targeted recommendations. Do not guarantee traffic before evidence exists.

## Q4. How is this different from ads or delivery platforms?
Haocu aims to match meals to personal taste and context, not only sell exposure. Over time, restaurants may benefit from structured demand insight and better menu discovery.

## Q5. What should be measured?
- restaurant onboarding time
- number of menu items structured
- customer saves/clicks/views
- recommendation appearances
- QR page scans if used
- user feedback on restaurant recommendation quality
- restaurant willingness to continue after pilot

## Q6. What legal/commercial issues need review?
- use of restaurant name, logo, menu, and photos
- nutrition estimate disclaimers
- pricing and promotional claims
- data sharing and analytics terms
- pilot duration and termination
- whether pilot is free, paid, or in-kind

## Q7. What is a good pilot commitment?
A simple LOI, email confirmation, or pilot participation note can be enough at the earliest stage. The key is clarity: what the restaurant allows, what Haocu will do, what data may be shown, and what success means.

## Q8. What should investors see?
Investors should see restaurant interest evidence such as meeting notes, signed/emailed pilot confirmations, menu setup screenshots, pilot results, and restaurant feedback. All evidence should be logged in the pilot tracker.
