## 1. Company Snapshot

**Company / Project:** Haocu.  
**Category:** AI-powered food decision, nutrition memory, restaurant recommendation, and meal-buddy social platform for Taiwan's outside-eating population.  
**Stage:** Demo / MVP planning and fundraising preparation.  
**Primary wedge:** Make daily meal decisions easier by combining meal photo analysis, personal taste memory, nutrition-aware recommendations, and low-pressure meal companionship.

Haocu is built around a simple insight: people do not only need calorie calculation. They need a system that remembers what they actually like, understands their daily eating context, helps them choose what to eat next, and can optionally connect them with compatible people around meals.

## 2. Problem

The Taiwan outside-eating user faces four recurring problems:

1. **Decision fatigue:** choosing what to eat every day is repetitive, emotional, and location-dependent.
2. **Generic reviews:** public restaurant ratings often reflect mass preference, influencer effects, or review-volume bias rather than one user's taste.
3. **Nutrition friction:** nutrition tracking is too much work for ordinary meals, especially when eating outside.
4. **Social friction:** people may want food companionship, but existing dating/social products are not designed around low-pressure meal context.

The result is a large daily-use behavior with weak personalization and high repetition.

## 3. Solution

Haocu combines four loops:

### A. AI Meal Analysis Loop
Users capture or upload a meal. The system estimates meal identity, ingredients, and nutrition, while allowing user correction. The correction becomes data for future personalization.

### B. Taste Memory Loop
Meal feedback, corrections, saved restaurants, and ratings become a private taste profile. The score is not designed as a public rating first; it is a personal recommendation memory.

### C. Next-Meal Recommendation Loop
The system recommends what to eat next based on recent intake, user taste, location, food type, budget/context, and available restaurants.

### D. Meal Buddy Loop
When users want companionship, Haocu can create a meal-buddy card from a meal or restaurant context and recommend compatible meal partners. Social matching is meal-first, not dating-first.

## 4. Why Now

The opportunity exists because several shifts are converging:

- mainstream users are more willing to use AI for everyday decisions
- phone-based food photography is already habitual
- restaurant discovery remains fragmented and review-biased
- wellness and nutrition interest is rising, but heavy tracking remains inconvenient
- loneliness and low-pressure companionship are real consumer needs
- restaurants need more direct, structured ways to understand demand beyond ads and reviews

Haocu's thesis is that food is one of the most frequent and emotionally rich daily decisions, making it an unusually strong wedge for AI personalization.

## 5. Product Scope

The MVP should focus on the smallest loop that can prove retention and recommendation value:

1. AI meal analysis and correction
2. daily intake / food diary
3. personal taste and rating memory
4. next-meal recommendation
5. restaurant discovery and selection
6. meal-buddy card creation
7. limited social matching and chat flow
8. basic premium differentiation
9. restaurant-side data foundation for later pilots

Non-MVP items such as supply-chain ESG, family meal planning, community cabinets, or advanced restaurant analytics should be framed as future expansion layers, not required for first validation.

## 6. Differentiation

Haocu differs from generic nutrition apps, restaurant review apps, and social apps because it sits between them:

| Category | What Existing Products Usually Do | Haocu Difference |
|---|---|---|
| Nutrition tracker | Log calories/macros after eating. | Uses meal recognition and correction to reduce friction and create meal memory. |
| Restaurant review app | Ranks restaurants by public reviews. | Builds private taste memory so recommendations can be more personally relevant. |
| Dating/social app | Matches people first, then activity. | Starts from meal intent and lowers social pressure. |
| Restaurant ads | Sell exposure. | Can evolve toward demand insight, menu nutrition, pilot traffic, and personalized discovery. |

The key product thesis is not "another AI calorie app". It is: **AI-powered personal food memory that can recommend meals and connect meal context to restaurants and people.**

## 7. Business Model

Potential revenue streams:

1. **Consumer Premium:** more meal-buddy cards, more candidates, longer food diary memory, taste insights, advanced goal mode, and enhanced social identity.
2. **Restaurant Tools:** menu/nutrition profile, verified restaurant cards, QR nutrition pages, campaign/pilot tools, and later analytics.
3. **Crowdfunding / Prelaunch:** early supporter packages, mascot IP, premium credits, restaurant campaign bundles.
4. **Future B2B / ESG:** supply-chain, near-expiry, meal-kit, and family nutrition logistics after MVP validation.

For the current stage, the investor memo should present Consumer Premium and restaurant pilot interest as the most concrete near-term paths, while keeping future supply-chain expansion as upside rather than the core initial dependency.

## 8. Go-To-Market

Recommended sequence:

### Phase 1 — Demo Readiness
- show a clean app walkthrough
- prove AI analysis → recommendation → meal-buddy flow
- prepare founder pitch, memo, data room, and diligence Q&A

### Phase 2 — MVP Pilot
- recruit a small controlled user group
- test meal analysis completion rate, correction rate, meal diary return rate, recommendation click/use rate, and meal-buddy card creation
- recruit restaurant pilot partners for menu/nutrition/data-card setup

### Phase 3 — Crowdfunding / Community Launch
- use food personality test, mascot IP, founder story, and early product demo to build demand
- validate willingness to pay and user acquisition messaging

### Phase 4 — Angel / Accelerator Follow-On
- use pilot metrics and crowdfunding signal to support fundraising
- refine MVP roadmap and hiring plan

## 12. Key Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Investor Concern | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Scope too broad | AI + nutrition + social + restaurant is too much. | Keep MVP loop narrow: AI analysis → taste memory → recommendation → optional meal buddy. |
| Nutrition accuracy | Users may treat estimates as medical advice. | Position as assistive estimate, allow correction, avoid medical claims, add review. |
| Social safety | Meal matching can create trust/safety issues. | Anonymous mode, verification tiers, reporting, limited invite flow, conservative MVP. |
| Restaurant adoption | Restaurants may not onboard. | Start with lightweight cards, pilot partners, value from visibility and nutrition transparency. |
| AI cost | AI analysis may be expensive. | Database-first policy, caching, structured menu data, correction reuse, cost monitoring. |
| Fundraising readiness | Investors need proof. | Alpha 9 evidence trackers force claims to be linked to demo, pilot, or verified data. |

## 14. Closing Thesis

Haocu has the potential to become the personal food intelligence layer for outside-eating users: a system that remembers taste, helps choose meals, makes nutrition less painful, and can turn eating into a low-pressure social activity. The first investable milestone is not to prove every long-term platform idea. It is to prove the daily loop: users analyze meals, correct and rate them, come back for recommendations, and optionally create meal social intent around food.
