## Haocu in One Sentence

Haocu is an AI-powered food decision platform for Taiwan's outside-eating users, combining meal photo analysis, private taste memory, nutrition-aware restaurant recommendations, and optional meal-buddy matching.

## Problem

Daily meal choice is frequent but poorly personalized. Public restaurant reviews do not know a user's taste, nutrition tracking is too much work, and existing social apps are not designed around low-pressure food companionship.

## Product

Haocu's MVP centers on four connected loops:

1. **AI meal analysis:** photo/upload → estimated meal/nutrition → user correction.
2. **Taste memory:** private ratings and corrections become recommendation data.
3. **Next-meal recommendation:** recommends what to eat next based on recent intake, taste, and location.
4. **Meal buddy:** optionally creates meal context cards to find compatible meal partners.

## Why It Is Different

Haocu is not just a calorie tracker, review app, or dating app. It uses food as the behavioral anchor:

- nutrition is assistive, not medical
- ratings are private personalization data, not public popularity contests
- social is meal-first and optional
- restaurants become structured data partners over time

## Business Model

Near-term revenue paths:

- consumer premium features
- restaurant pilot tools and verified profile features
- crowdfunding / early supporter packages

Longer-term upside:

- restaurant analytics
- supply-chain / ESG workflows
- family nutrition and meal planning products

## Current Stage

Haocu has a structured product repository, PRD, engineering backlog, professional review pack, fundraising pack, clean data room, and now a diligence-ready investor memo / Q&A / evidence tracking pack. The next milestone is MVP execution and controlled pilot evidence.

## What We Need to Prove

1. Users complete meal analysis and correction.
2. Users return for food diary and next-meal recommendations.
3. Recommendation quality improves with taste memory.
4. Meal-buddy cards generate social intent without overwhelming the product.
5. Restaurants are willing to provide menu/data/pilot participation.
6. Users show willingness to pay or support the product through premium/crowdfunding.

## Ask

Seeking capital, advisors, and restaurant pilot partners to support MVP build, professional review, pilot execution, and market validation. Exact fundraising terms should be inserted only after finance/legal review.
