# Finance and Fundraising Q&A

> Boundary: Not financial, tax, accounting, securities, valuation, or investment advice. Use as a preparation framework.

## Q1. How much is Haocu raising?
Insert only the current reviewed fundraising amount. If the amount is still being evaluated, say the company is preparing a milestone-based seed/angel raise to fund MVP, pilot, review, and launch testing.

## Q2. What will the money be used for?
Primary categories:

- MVP engineering
- AI/recommendation iteration
- UI/UX polish and demo readiness
- professional legal/privacy/finance review
- restaurant pilot operations
- user testing and growth experiments
- crowdfunding/prelaunch execution
- contingency and founder runway

## Q3. What milestones should the raise buy?
- stable MVP demo / closed beta
- user pilot metrics
- restaurant pilot confirmations
- recommendation and diary retention evidence
- meal-buddy engagement evidence
- reviewed legal/privacy/health-claim surfaces
- crowdfunding or willingness-to-pay signal

## Q4. What are the main cost risks?
- AI inference and image storage
- engineering velocity and contractor cost
- restaurant onboarding labor
- customer support and trust/safety
- paid acquisition inefficiency
- professional review cost
- infrastructure scaling

## Q5. What revenue comes first?
Likely first tests are consumer premium willingness-to-pay, crowdfunding early supporters, and restaurant pilot interest. Do not model later supply-chain/ESG revenue as required for MVP survival.

## Q6. What metrics should finance track early?
- CAC by channel
- activation rate
- retention by cohort
- AI cost per analysis
- storage cost per active user
- premium conversion intent
- restaurant onboarding cost
- gross margin assumptions under different AI usage levels

## Q7. What should not be sent casually?
- raw cap table
- unreviewed valuation math
- sensitive founder financial information
- investor-specific negotiated terms
- unreviewed tax/securities conclusions
- personal guarantees or debt details unless intentionally disclosed and reviewed

## Q8. How should investors evaluate the ask?
Investors should evaluate whether the requested capital is enough to reach the next proof point, not whether it can fund the full long-term platform. A milestone-based plan is the cleanest framing.
