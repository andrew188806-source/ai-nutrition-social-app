# Risk Response and Red Flags

> Use: Direct, honest answers for investor risk discussions.

## Top Risks

| Risk | Why It Matters | Clean Response | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scope creep | Investors may think AI + social + restaurant + ESG is too broad. | The MVP is narrowed to the daily food loop; later layers are roadmap upside. | Prioritize AI analysis, diary, recommendation, limited meal buddy. |
| AI/nutrition accuracy | Wrong estimates can reduce trust or create liability. | Estimates are assistive, editable, and not medical advice. | User correction, database-first data, disclaimers, professional review. |
| Social safety | Matching strangers can create trust/safety risks. | Social is optional and bounded in early versions. | Anonymous mode, verification, reporting, limited invite flow. |
| Weak retention | Meal analysis may be a novelty, not a habit. | The pilot must prove repeat use. | Track D1/D7, diary saves, recommendation actions. |
| Restaurant adoption | Restaurants may not provide data or pay. | Start lightweight and validate willingness through pilots. | LOI tracker, onboarding time, value proposition testing. |
| AI cost | Image analysis can be expensive at scale. | Cost controls are built into architecture. | Database-first, caching, limits, premium gating, monitoring. |
| Legal/fundraising complexity | Cross-border, IP, privacy, securities topics require care. | Repository is prepared for review, not legal conclusion. | Use professional review pack and counsel before external claims. |

## Red Flags Investors May Raise

### "This is too many products."
Best response: Agree that the full platform is broad, then emphasize the MVP wedge. The first milestone is not to build everything; it is to prove a daily AI food decision loop.

### "Nutrition estimates are risky."
Best response: Haocu is not positioned as medical advice. The user can correct outputs, and public claims will be conservative and reviewed.

### "Social matching could be dangerous or distracting."
Best response: Meal-buddy is optional, limited, and phased. The product can still work as AI food memory/recommendation without making social the only core value.

### "Restaurants will not pay."
Best response: That is exactly what pilots should validate. The first restaurant goal is low-friction data/interest evidence, not immediate enterprise SaaS scale.

### "Where is the traction?"
Best response: Current stage is demo/MVP preparation. Alpha 9 explicitly tracks what evidence exists and what must be proven next. The fundraising ask should be milestone-based.

## Internal Red-Flag Actions

- If the same concern appears in 3+ investor calls, update the memo.
- If a concern touches legal/medical/privacy/securities, add it to professional review.
- If investors misunderstand the product category, simplify the first 30 seconds.
- If investors ask for metrics that do not exist yet, add them to the pilot evidence plan.
