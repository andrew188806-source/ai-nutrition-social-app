# Founding Team and Hiring Q&A

## Q1. What team does Haocu need first?
The first team should be milestone-based:

1. technical owner / senior full-stack or mobile engineer
2. AI/data support for meal analysis and recommendation logic
3. product/design support for clean MVP UX
4. legal/privacy/health-claim professional review
5. restaurant pilot operator or founder-led BD support
6. growth/crowdfunding support once demo is ready

## Q2. Should contractors receive equity?
Equity should generally be reserved for committed core team members, not ordinary vendors, unless the contractor is taking meaningful founder-level risk and responsibilities. This should be handled through legal agreements.

## Q3. What founder responsibilities remain non-delegable?
- product vision and scope control
- investor story and fundraising process
- restaurant/user discovery
- prioritization and final tradeoff decisions
- brand/community tone
- legal/finance decision ownership with professional advisors

## Q4. What can be delegated early?
- implementation tasks
- QA/test plans
- design polish
- analytics instrumentation
- restaurant data entry workflows
- campaign asset production
- documentation cleanup

## Q5. What is the hiring milestone logic?
Do not hire ahead of proof. Hire or contract for the next bottleneck:

| Milestone | Hiring Need |
|---|---|
| Demo unstable | engineering support |
| Users confused | product/design support |
| AI quality weak | AI/data support |
| Investor legal questions | counsel/reviewer |
| Restaurant pilots start | BD/ops support |
| Crowdfunding launches | content/growth support |

## Q6. What should investors worry about?
Investors may worry that the product is too broad for one founder. The answer is disciplined scope: prove the MVP loop, delegate specialized work, and postpone expansion layers.
