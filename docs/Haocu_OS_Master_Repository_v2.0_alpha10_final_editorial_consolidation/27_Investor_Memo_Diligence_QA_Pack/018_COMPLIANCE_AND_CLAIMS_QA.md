# Compliance and Claims Q&A

> Boundary: This is not legal advice. Use it to prepare conservative investor answers and professional review questions.

## Q1. Can Haocu say it improves health?
Avoid broad health improvement claims unless reviewed and supported. Safer language: Haocu helps users understand meals, remember preferences, and make more informed food decisions.

## Q2. Can Haocu give nutrition numbers?
Yes as estimates if presented carefully, editable by users, and not framed as medical or diagnostic. Restaurant-provided data should be distinguished from AI-estimated data.

## Q3. Can Haocu recommend diets for medical conditions?
Not in MVP unless professionally reviewed and carefully scoped. Avoid disease treatment, diagnosis, or medical nutrition therapy claims.

## Q4. What data needs special care?
Food photos, nutrition records, user preferences, location context, social profiles, chats/invites, verification records, and any health-adjacent data.

## Q5. What public claims should be avoided?
- guaranteed weight loss
- medical benefit claims
- guaranteed AI accuracy
- guaranteed restaurant traffic
- guaranteed investment returns
- exclusive patent protection unless reviewed
- claims that all user data is safe without security evidence

## Q6. What investor materials need review before broad sharing?
- fundraising terms
- securities/crowdfunding statements
- patent/IP claims
- privacy policy and terms
- health/nutrition wording
- restaurant commercial terms
- financial projections and valuation math

## Q7. What is the safe investor framing?
Haocu is a product and market validation opportunity. The current repository is structured for review, and sensitive claims are marked for professional validation.
