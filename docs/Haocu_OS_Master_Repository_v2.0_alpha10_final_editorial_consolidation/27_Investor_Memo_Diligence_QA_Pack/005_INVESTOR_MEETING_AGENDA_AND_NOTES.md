## Recommended 30-Minute Agenda

| Time | Topic | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3 min | Founder and context | Establish why this problem and why now. |
| 3–8 min | Problem and user | Confirm investor understands outside-eating / food decision pain. |
| 8–15 min | Product demo | Show AI analysis → taste memory → recommendation → meal buddy. |
| 15–20 min | Business model and GTM | Show how MVP can become a business. |
| 20–25 min | Evidence and milestones | Be clear what is proven vs planned. |
| 25–30 min | Ask and next steps | Confirm diligence requests and follow-up owner. |

## Questions to Ask Investor

1. Which part of the Haocu thesis is most compelling or least clear?
2. Would you evaluate this more as consumer AI, food discovery, social, restaurant SaaS, or marketplace?
3. What evidence would you need before writing a check or making an intro?
4. Do you know restaurant partners, food creators, angels, or operators who should see this?
5. Are there specific legal/compliance or trust/safety concerns you would want reviewed early?
