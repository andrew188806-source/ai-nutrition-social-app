# ESG / Supply Chain Review

## Review Goal

Prepare the Stage 2 surplus-food / ESG / supply-chain concept for later review without overcommitting the MVP. Alpha 7B treats ESG as a future phase that requires separate legal, food-safety, logistics, and insurance review.

## Stage 2 Concept

- Restaurants or food providers may sell surplus or near-expiry food at discount.
- Haocu may help match demand, improve prediction, and route inventory.
- Future B2B workflows may include cold-chain pickup, dynamic pricing, and community pickup points.
- ESG impact claims may include reduced waste only if measured and substantiated.

## Review Questions

### Food Safety

- What licenses, handling procedures, storage rules, and temperature logs are required?
- Who is responsible if food becomes unsafe after pickup or resale?
- Are there restrictions on selling near-expiry meals?
- What labeling is required for allergens, preparation time, expiry, and storage?

### Logistics

- Can cold-chain pickup be outsourced?
- What partner agreements are needed?
- Who owns risk during transport?
- What insurance is required?

### ESG Claims

- What data is needed before claiming reduced food waste?
- Can the app claim ESG benefit without third-party audit?
- What metrics must be tracked: kilograms saved, meals redistributed, emissions estimate, disposal reduction?
- What disclaimers are needed for estimates?

### Consumer Protection

- What refund/dispute rules are needed for discounted surplus food?
- What warnings are needed about pickup window and storage?
- What restrictions apply to vulnerable users or special diets?

## MVP Boundary

Do not include Stage 2 ESG/surplus-food operations in MVP launch commitments. It may appear as roadmap, vision, or future phase only after professional review.
