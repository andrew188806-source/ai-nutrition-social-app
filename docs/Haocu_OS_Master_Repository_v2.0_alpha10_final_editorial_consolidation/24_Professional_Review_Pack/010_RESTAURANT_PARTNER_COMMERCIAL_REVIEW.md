# Restaurant Partner Commercial Review

## Review Goal

Validate whether the restaurant-side value proposition, onboarding flow, data responsibility, pricing, partnership terms, sponsored placement, and operational promises are clear enough for a first pilot.

## Restaurant Value Proposition To Review

- Restaurants get menu nutrition visibility without building their own AI system.
- Verified restaurant data can improve user trust and recommendation accuracy.
- Meal Buddy and group-table flows can drive dine-in demand.
- Restaurant pages can show dishes, tags, nutrition estimates, social context, and potentially sponsorships.
- Future ESG/surplus-food features may open a B2B channel.

## Commercial Questions

- What is the simplest restaurant pilot package?
- Should restaurants pay before user traction exists, or should early partners be free/discounted pilots?
- What proof is needed before asking for monthly fees?
- What restaurant KPIs should be reported: views, saves, card creation, group tables, visits, redemptions?
- What data can be shared with restaurants without violating user privacy?

## Contract Questions

- Who owns uploaded restaurant menu data and food photos?
- Who is responsible for inaccurate price/menu/nutrition information?
- What approval is required before using restaurant name/logo in pitch/crowdfunding materials?
- What happens if a restaurant closes, changes menu, or disputes a user-uploaded dish?
- What terms govern sponsored recommendation or featured placement?

## Operational Questions

- What is the minimum onboarding checklist?
- How should restaurant verification work?
- Who reviews menu/nutrition edits?
- How are complaints handled?
- What support SLA is realistic for MVP?

## Pilot Recommendation

For MVP, keep restaurant partner commitments narrow:

1. Menu listing and dish data.
2. AI-assisted nutrition estimate clearly labeled as estimated.
3. Restaurant profile and selected dishes.
4. No guaranteed customer volume.
5. No ESG/surplus-food commitments until Stage 2 review.
