# Cross-Border Taiwan / US Review

## Review Goal

Prepare questions for advisors when the founder, company structure, IP ownership, investors, cloud vendors, AI vendors, or taxes involve both Taiwan and the United States.

## Company / Founder Questions

- Should the operating company be Taiwan-based, US-based, or structured with both?
- What are the tax and reporting implications if the founder has US tax obligations while operating a Taiwan company?
- What annual filings, bank, payroll, and accounting systems are required?
- How should founder loans, founder IP licensing, and contractor payments be documented?

## Fundraising Questions

- Can Taiwan and US investors invest in the same company easily?
- What entity structure is easiest for angel/VC fundraising?
- What securities counsel is needed before taking US investor money?
- How should crowdfunding be separated from investment fundraising?
- What investor materials should differ between Taiwan and US audiences?

## IP Questions

- Where should patents/trademarks be filed first?
- Is a Taiwan first filing enough before US or international strategy?
- If the founder personally owns IP, how does that affect fundraising, tax, and company valuation?
- What assignments/licenses are needed before taking investment?

## Data / Privacy Questions

- What is disclosed if Taiwan user data is processed by US-based cloud or AI vendors?
- Are vendor DPAs required?
- What data-transfer language should be in the privacy policy?
- What is the breach notification workflow if vendors are cross-border?

## Practical Output Requested

- Recommended entity/fundraising path.
- Founder tax/accounting checklist.
- Cross-border IP filing checklist.
- Data-transfer disclosure checklist.
- Red flags before approaching US investors.

## Boundary

This is a review prompt only. Cross-border tax, securities, corporate, IP, and privacy decisions require qualified professional advice.
