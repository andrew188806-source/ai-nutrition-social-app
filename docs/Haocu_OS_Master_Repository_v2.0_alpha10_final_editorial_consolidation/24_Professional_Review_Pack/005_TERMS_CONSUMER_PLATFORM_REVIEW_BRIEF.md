# Terms / Consumer Platform Review Brief

## Review Goal

Define the user-facing legal terms, platform rules, moderation powers, restaurant responsibilities, and consumer-protection boundaries required for MVP.

## Surfaces Requiring Terms

- Mobile app onboarding.
- AI meal photo analysis.
- Today intake / food diary.
- Meal Buddy card creation.
- Anonymous and verified social cards.
- Matching, invitations, chat, and group tables.
- Premium subscription and feature limits.
- Restaurant recommendation and restaurant detail pages.
- Restaurant admin portal.
- User feedback, ratings, saved meals, and shared posts.
- Crowdfunding or pre-sale reward fulfillment.

## Terms Questions

### Consumer App

- What must users agree to before using AI analysis and nutrition estimate features?
- What rules are needed for uploading photos and user-generated content?
- How should paid features, free limits, trial access, refund rules, and feature changes be disclosed?
- What disclaimers are needed for recommendation accuracy, restaurant information, and availability?

### Community / Social

- What conduct rules are required for chat, matching, meal meetups, group tables, reporting, and account suspension?
- Should Haocu disclaim responsibility for offline meetups?
- Should users be warned to meet in public locations and avoid sharing sensitive information?
- What enforcement actions should Haocu reserve?

### Restaurants

- What merchant agreement terms are needed for menu uploads, photos, nutrition estimates, sponsored placement, verification badges, and data corrections?
- Can Haocu edit or remove restaurant content?
- How are restaurant disputes with users handled?

### AI and Nutrition

- What terms clarify that nutrition estimates are approximate and informational?
- What terms prevent reliance for medical diagnosis, disease treatment, eating disorder management, pregnancy nutrition, or clinical diet planning?
- What escalation language is needed for users with medical conditions?

## Policy Documents To Draft After Review

- Terms of Service.
- Privacy Policy.
- Community Guidelines.
- AI/Nutrition Disclaimer.
- Restaurant Partner Terms.
- Sponsored Content / Advertising Policy.
- Content Moderation Policy.
- User Safety and Offline Meetup Policy.
