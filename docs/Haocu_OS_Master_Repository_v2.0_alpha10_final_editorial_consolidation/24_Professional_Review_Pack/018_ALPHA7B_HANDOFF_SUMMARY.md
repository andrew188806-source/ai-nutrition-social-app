# Alpha 7B Handoff Summary

## What Alpha 7B Adds

Alpha 7B adds a professional-review layer on top of the master repository and Alpha 7A engineering backlog.

It prepares Haocu for external review by:

- Separating reviewer types.
- Mapping repository sections to reviewer briefs.
- Turning vague review needs into specific questions.
- Identifying red/yellow/green risk boundaries.
- Providing reusable reviewer outreach messages.
- Creating a decision-log format for turning advice into tasks.
- Creating importable `review_questions.csv` and `review_questions.json`.

## Do Not Skip

Before public launch or serious fundraising, obtain review for:

- AI nutrition disclaimers.
- Privacy policy and consent flow.
- Terms of service/community guidelines.
- Restaurant partner terms.
- Crowdfunding/fundraising wording.
- IP/patent disclosure strategy.
- Security readiness for real user data.
- Finance/accounting/tax setup.

## Practical Use

Send only the relevant brief to each reviewer. Do not send the whole repository unless requested. Use the decision log to keep feedback actionable.
