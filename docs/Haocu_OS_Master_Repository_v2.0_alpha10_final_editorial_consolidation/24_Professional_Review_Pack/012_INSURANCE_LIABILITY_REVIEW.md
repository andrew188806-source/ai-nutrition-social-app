# Insurance / Liability Review

## Review Goal

Identify insurance coverage and liability controls needed for a food + AI + social + restaurant platform before launch and before fundraising claims are made.

## Potential Coverage Areas

- General commercial liability.
- Professional liability / errors and omissions.
- Cyber liability.
- Directors and officers liability after fundraising or formal board formation.
- Employment practices liability after hiring.
- Product liability if selling physical goods or mascot rewards.
- Event liability if hosting offline meal events.
- Food safety liability if Stage 2 surplus-food operations launch.

## Risk Areas

### AI/Nutrition

- Users rely on inaccurate nutrition estimates.
- Users treat app output as medical advice.
- Restaurant nutrition data is wrong.

### Social / Offline Meetup

- Users meet strangers through the app.
- Harassment, disputes, no-shows, scams, or unsafe interactions occur.
- Group tables create expectations Haocu does not control.

### Restaurant / Food

- Restaurant info is inaccurate.
- Allergens are omitted.
- User gets sick after choosing a restaurant through the app.
- Stage 2 surplus-food logistics creates food safety exposure.

### Data / Security

- Photo, chat, profile, or health-goal data is exposed.
- Account takeover.
- Admin misuse.

### Fundraising / Governance

- Investor disputes over claims, valuation, use of funds, or IP ownership.
- Crowdfunding reward delay.

## Questions For Insurance Broker / Counsel

- What minimum coverage should exist before public launch?
- What coverage should exist before accepting investor funds?
- Are AI/nutrition recommendations covered or excluded?
- Are offline meetups covered or excluded?
- Are restaurant partner disputes covered?
- Are cyber events covered if Supabase or an AI vendor is involved?
- What exclusions must be understood before Stage 2 food operations?

## Controls To Reduce Risk

- Strong disclaimers.
- Community guidelines.
- Reporting/blocking/moderation.
- Private-by-default photos.
- RLS and audit logs.
- Restaurant data responsibility clauses.
- No medical claims.
- No Stage 2 food handling before separate review.
