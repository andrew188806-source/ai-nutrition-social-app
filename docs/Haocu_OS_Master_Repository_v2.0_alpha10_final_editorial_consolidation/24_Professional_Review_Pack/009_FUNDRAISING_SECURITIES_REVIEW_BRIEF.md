# Fundraising / Securities Review Brief

## Review Goal

Ensure that Haocu's crowdfunding, angel fundraising, investor deck, one-pager, data room, valuation discussion, reward tiers, and public claims do not create securities, advertising, consumer-protection, or misrepresentation issues.

## Materials To Review

- Pitch deck outline.
- One-pager.
- Investor FAQ.
- Investor memo.
- Crowdfunding reward packages.
- Use-of-funds plan.
- Milestone plan.
- Risk disclosures.
- Cap table and equity boundary.
- Patent/IP valuation language.
- Restaurant traction or pilot claims.

## Questions For Fundraising Counsel / Advisor

### Crowdfunding

- Is the campaign rewards-based, securities-based, donation-based, or pre-sale?
- What claims are allowed about future app access, perks, restaurant benefits, mascot rewards, and premium accounts?
- What refund/fulfillment disclosures are required?
- What happens if MVP delivery is delayed?

### Angel / Investor Outreach

- What investor-disclosure language is required?
- What claims about market size, traction, technology, patents, AI accuracy, and valuation should be softened?
- What materials should be marked confidential?
- What data room content is safe to share before formal IP filing?

### Securities Boundary

- Can public materials mention investment upside?
- What cannot be said to general supporters?
- When does the company need counsel before accepting funds?
- What offering structure is appropriate for Taiwan angels, US angels, or cross-border investors?

### IP / Valuation

- Can pending patent strategy be used in valuation discussions?
- How should founder-owned IP licensed to company be disclosed?
- What documents must exist before claiming IP protection strengthens valuation?

## Review Output Needed

- Redline or approval notes for pitch language.
- Required crowdfunding disclaimers.
- Investor-risk disclosure list.
- Safe public claim bank.
- Unsafe claim list.
- Funding instrument recommendation boundary.
- Data room sensitive-document guidance.
