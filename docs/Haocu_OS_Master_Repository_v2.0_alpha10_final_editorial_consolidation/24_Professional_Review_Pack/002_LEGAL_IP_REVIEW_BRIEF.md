# Legal / IP Review Brief

## Review Goal

Confirm that the Haocu product, business model, brand, contractor/IP structure, user terms, restaurant terms, and fundraising materials are legally reviewable and do not create avoidable ownership or liability risk.

## Legal Questions

### Product / Platform

- Is Haocu acting as a content platform, recommendation platform, marketplace, social platform, restaurant marketing channel, or some combination?
- What disclaimers are needed for AI nutrition estimates, restaurant recommendations, social matching, and user-generated content?
- Should the app prohibit medical, eating-disorder, body-transformation, or disease-treatment use cases in the terms?
- Are free vs premium feature differences adequately disclosed?

### User Terms

- What terms are required for consumer accounts, meal-photo uploads, AI analysis, meal records, social cards, matching, chat, group tables, ratings, and referrals?
- What moderation and removal rights must Haocu reserve?
- What community rules are required for anonymous vs verified real-person profiles?
- What age gate is needed?

### Restaurant Terms

- Who is responsible for accuracy of restaurant menu, pricing, ingredient, nutrition, allergen, and sponsored information?
- Can restaurants claim verified nutrition without third-party lab or dietitian review?
- What disclosures are required for sponsored recommendations, ads, or paid placement?
- What merchant termination or suspension clauses are needed?

### IP Ownership

- Do all contractors, designers, engineers, AI prompt writers, mascot creators, and consultants need invention/IP assignment agreements?
- Should the founder personally own patents/trademarks and license them to the company, or should the company own them directly?
- What are the risks of licensing founder-owned IP to the company while fundraising?
- What records should be retained for invention dates, code ownership, and design provenance?

## Deliverables Requested From Counsel

- Required terms-of-service sections.
- Privacy-policy cross-check items.
- IP assignment checklist.
- Contractor agreement checklist.
- Restaurant agreement clause list.
- Fundraising disclaimer and securities boundary notes.
- Blockers before demo, crowdfunding, or investor outreach.
