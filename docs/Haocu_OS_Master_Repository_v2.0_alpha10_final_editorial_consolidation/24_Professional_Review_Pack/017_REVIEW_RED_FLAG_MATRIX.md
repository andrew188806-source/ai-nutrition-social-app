# Professional Review Red Flag Matrix

## Purpose

This matrix helps decide what cannot be launched, published, or promised before review.

| Area | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| AI Nutrition | Approximate estimates with disclaimer and correction flow | Confidence display missing | Medical, disease, or guaranteed body-result claims |
| Privacy | Consent, retention, deletion, and vendor disclosures drafted | Data export not automated yet | No privacy policy before storing real photos/chats |
| Photos | Private storage and deletion flow | Deletion manual but documented | Public buckets or persistent unreviewed AI vendor logs |
| Social Matching | Community rules, reporting, blocking, safety copy | Moderation manual | No safety rules for meeting strangers |
| Chat | Participant-only access and retention policy | Retention unclear | Users can access non-participant chats |
| Restaurant Data | Estimated nutrition labeled and restaurant responsibility defined | Verification process manual | Claims of verified/accurate nutrition without review |
| Sponsored Content | Paid placement labeled | Labeling copy incomplete | Hidden ads or undisclosed sponsorship |
| Crowdfunding | Reward terms, delivery risk, refund rules disclosed | Fulfillment cost uncertain | Investment-like public claims in rewards campaign |
| Investor Materials | Risks and assumptions disclosed | Valuation support weak | False traction, patent, or revenue claims |
| IP | Contractor assignment process exists | Some old work lacks signatures | Public invention disclosure before filing decision |
| Security | RLS/storage/secret policies reviewed | Tests incomplete | Service key exposed or private data readable |
| Finance | Basic accounting and founder loans documented | Dashboard not automated | Mixing personal/company funds without records |
| ESG Stage 2 | Roadmap only | Pilot being researched | Food logistics launched without food safety/legal/insurance review |
| Cross-Border | Advisor review scheduled | Structure undecided | Accepting US/Taiwan investor funds without counsel |

## Red Flag Rule

Any red item becomes a blocker for the relevant launch path:

- Product launch blocker.
- Restaurant pilot blocker.
- Crowdfunding blocker.
- Investor outreach blocker.
- Patent disclosure blocker.
- Stage 2 blocker.

Blockers should be logged in `016_REVIEW_DECISION_LOG_TEMPLATE.md` and converted to tasks in the engineering or legal backlog.
