# Finance / Accounting / Tax Review Brief

## Review Goal

Confirm that the MVP plan, budget, pricing, subscriptions, crowdfunding rewards, restaurant revenue, equity planning, and runway assumptions can be tracked cleanly and do not create avoidable tax/accounting risk.

## Finance Topics

- MVP development budget.
- AI/API/cloud cost control.
- Subscription pricing.
- Crowdfunding reward revenue recognition.
- Restaurant fees, sponsorship, and future B2B revenue.
- Contractor payments and invoices.
- Founder loans and reimbursement.
- Cap table planning.
- Patent/IP expenses.
- Mascot merchandise and reward fulfillment costs.
- Company setup and accounting system.
- Taiwan and possible US tax complexity.

## Accounting Questions

- How should crowdfunding receipts be recorded: revenue, deferred revenue, liability, or pre-sale obligation?
- How should founder loans and reimbursements be documented?
- What invoices/contracts are needed for contractors?
- What costs can be capitalized vs expensed?
- How should app subscription revenue and platform fees be tracked?
- What chart of accounts is recommended for MVP?

## Tax Questions

- What Taiwan tax obligations apply after company formation?
- Are there US tax filing/reporting issues if a US citizen founder owns/operates a Taiwan company?
- What tax treatment applies to patent ownership/licensing if founder owns IP personally?
- What taxes or reporting apply to overseas angel investment?
- What tax issues apply to merchandise/crowdfunding reward fulfillment?

## Fundraising Finance Questions

- Is the proposed use of funds internally consistent?
- Is the burn rate realistic?
- What evidence should support valuation?
- How should convertible notes, SAFE-like instruments, direct equity, or founder loans be compared?
- What minimum finance dashboard should be maintained monthly?

## Deliverables Requested

- MVP finance checklist.
- Accounting setup checklist.
- Tax-risk list.
- Founder loan/equity documentation checklist.
- Crowdfunding revenue-recognition guidance.
- Recommended monthly finance dashboard.
- Budget categories for investor data room.
