# 3-Minute Investor Demo Script

1. Open Mobile Consumer App and show Demo Access.
2. Start with meal photo flow and choose 外食模式 or 自己動手做模式.
3. Show AI/mock nutrition estimate and restaurant-aware menu identification.
4. Confirm or correct the result and save into Food Memory.
5. Show next-meal recommendation and tag reasoning.
6. Create a Community Card from the meal record.
7. Show nearby meal buddy matching, Free/Premium difference and profile unlock.
8. Show four-person table as an optional restaurant-based branch.
9. Show verified restaurant recommendation and sponsored labeling.
10. Open Restaurant Dashboard to show why restaurants join.
11. Open Admin Dashboard to show ESG, consent, review, tag and social safety governance.

Closing line: 好初不是單純 calorie tracker，而是 AI-powered social eating platform with a tag and governance moat.
## Updated 3-Minute Demo Flow

1. Open the Mobile Consumer App home. Explain the dual-core story: AI nutrition calculation plus social eating discovery.
2. Select a meal or restaurant and run mock AI nutrition analysis.
3. Show the meal result screen: calories, protein, carbs, fat, tags, and health-goal alignment.
4. Save the result into Food Memory.
5. Show the simulated delayed feedback card. Explain that production timing can be 30 minutes, 1 hour, dinner-time, or push follow-up.
6. Complete quick feedback. This unlocks next-meal recommendation, restaurant recommendation, nearby matching, Community Card, meal buddies, and four-person table options.
7. Trigger `根據這餐找飯友` or `根據這間餐廳找飯友`. Explain that the app does not show random people by default.
8. Open Community Card and show food-social profile, compatibility reason, and meal payment preference.
9. Send invite, open 飯友列表, then open mock chat and meal planning CTA.
10. Show restaurant recommendation and four-person table. Mention the AA-only group rule.
11. Open Restaurant Dashboard and Admin Dashboard to prove restaurant value, sponsored transparency, verification, and ESG governance.

## Final Navigation Talking Points

Use the bottom nav in this order: 首頁, AI分析, 飯友, 餐廳, 我的.

Say clearly that AI分析 is a main tab because nutrition calculation is not secondary. It creates Food Memory, Food Memory powers recommendations, and recommendations lead into contextual social dining.

When showing 飯友, explain that direct search exists as a shortcut, but higher-quality matching appears after meal analysis or restaurant selection.

When showing AI分析, tap an ingredient row to show inline correction, then add a missing ingredient. Emphasize that the user only fixes what AI got wrong, instead of filling out a complicated nutrition form.
