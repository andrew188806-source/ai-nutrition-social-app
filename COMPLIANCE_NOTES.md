# Compliance Notes

- This is lifestyle nutrition guidance, not medical diagnosis.
- Nutrition estimates must be labeled as estimates.
- Self-cooked estimates are not diagnosis or treatment advice.
- Exercise-linked recommendations are not medical or sports prescription advice.
- Verified restaurant badge means identity verification, nutrition disclosure participation and platform review only.
- Sponsored content must always be clearly labeled.
- Sponsored tags must be distinguishable from organic tags.
- Relationship/social intent tags are optional user-controlled preference data.
- Privacy settings must control social cards, restaurant social match cards and four-person table recommendations.
- Chat, friend graph and meal invite systems need production moderation, blocking, reporting and audit trails before launch.
- Push notifications require explicit consent and quiet-hour controls.
- Admin governance should log consent, data access, review actions and social safety actions.
## Phase 6 Compliance Corrections

Nutrition analysis, self-cooked estimates, and exercise-linked suggestions are lifestyle guidance only. They are not medical diagnosis, treatment advice, sports prescription, or clinical nutrition plans.

Delayed meal feedback should be consent-aware and configurable in production. Push notification reminders require explicit notification consent and clear opt-out controls.

Meal payment preference is not payment processing. The MVP must not imply bill settlement, escrow, money transfer, or legal payment responsibility.

Four-person tables are permanently AA-only in the MVP: `本餐桌採 AA 制，特殊消費請自行協調`. Do not add AB splitting, treating logic, alcohol preference, age checks, or bill-splitting features in Phase 6.

Social discovery must not be positioned as dating-first. Relationship/social intent labels are user-controlled preference data and must respect privacy settings.

Community Card is a nearby food matching profile, not a public social feed.
